# EEG and Sleep

## Electro Encephalography (EEG)
- Records brain waves
  - Summation of local potentials
  - = overall electrical activity of brain
### Characteristics of EEG waves
- Frequency
  - Number of waves / second
  - Frequency ∝ brain activity
- Amplitude
  - Height of wave
### Types
- α wave
  - Wave of inattention → relaxed state
  - 8–12 Hz
  - Source → occipital & parietal cortex
- β wave
  - Wave of attention / wakefulness
  - 13–30 Hz
  - Source → prefrontal cortex
- θ wave
  - Memory, sleep
  - 4–7 Hz
  - Source → hippocampus (short term → long term memory)
- δ wave
  - Deep sleep
  - 1–3 Hz → slowest
- γ wave
  - Focused attention
  - More than 60 Hz → max
- α block
  - Replacement of α wave with β wave

## Sleep
### NREM sleep
- Deep sleep
  - ↑ delta activity
- Stage 1 → N1
  - 4–7 Hz → theta
- Stage 2 → N2
  - 8–12 Hz
  - Sleep spindle on sinusoidal wave
  - K-complex → biphasic wave
- Stages 3 & 4 → N3 (deep sleep)
  - Delta appears in stage 3
  - Max delta activity in stage 4
- Physiological changes
  - RR ↓
  - BP ↓
  - Temperature ↓↓
  - Respiration slow & deep
### REM sleep
- EEG → β wave
- AKA paradoxical sleep
  - EEG resembles wakefulness
- Eyeball movement → PGO spikes
  - Pons
  - → (+) lateral geniculate body → magnocellular pathway
  - → (+) occipital lobe
  - → eyeball movement
- Physiological changes
  - Muscle atonia → prevents acting out of dreams
    - Exception → diaphragm (respiration)
    - Exception → extra ocular muscles (eyeball movement)
  - Genital organ (penis / clitoris) enlargement
- Sleep study record
  - Electro oculogram → eyeball movement
  - Electromyograph → no activity
### Hormonal changes
- Growth hormone → ↑ in stages 3 & 4 of NREM
- Prolactin → ↑ in REM sleep
- Luteinizing hormone → nocturnal LH surge in puberty
- Melatonin → sleep-inducing, secreted by pineal gland
### Dreams
- Duration
  - NREM → shorter
  - REM → longer (early morning)
- Type of dream
  - NREM → conceptual
  - REM → emotional
- Content of dream
  - NREM → related to day to day activities
  - REM → unrelated to day to day activities
### Sleep study — hypnogram
- 1 cycle = 1 REM + 1 NREM = 90 mins
  - Ultradian rhythm
- Within a cycle
  - 1 → sleep deepens
  - 2 → sleep lightens
  - 3 → REM
- Towards morning sleep lightens
  - Less stages 3 & 4
- Duration
  - Normal → NREM 75 % (stage 2 → 45 %)
  - Normal → REM 25 %
  - Neonate → REM 50 %
  - Elders → less deep sleep
### Regulation of sleep
- Areas promoting wakefulness
  - RAS → norepinephrine → widespread brain activation
  - Raphe nuclei → serotonin
  - Lateral hypothalamus → orexin
  - Posterior hypothalamus → histamine
  - EEG alerting response → high frequency stimulation of RAS → awakens
- Areas promoting sleep
  - Hypothalamus → ventrolateral pre optic nucleus (VLPON)
  - Cholinergic neurons → acetylcholine → facilitates REM sleep
  - Pineal gland → melatonin
- Mechanism of NREM
  - NREM-on neurons → GABA
  - → posterior hypothalamus
  - → histamine release inhibited
  - → NREM sleep
- Mechanism of REM
  - REM-on neurons → acetylcholine → REM sleep
### Parasomnias
- NREM parasomnias
  - Sleep walking / somnambulism → stages 3 & 4 (deep sleep)
  - Sleep talking
  - Bruxism / teeth grinding → stage 2
    - Seen in emotionally neglected children
  - Nocturnal enuresis / bed wetting
  - Night terrors / pavor nocturnus
- REM parasomnias
  - Narcolepsy → ↑ day time sleepiness
  - Nightmares → terror dreams in REM
