# Cardiac Cycle

## Cardiac cycle — duration
- Total duration → 0.8 s
- Atrial systole → 0.1 s
- Atrial diastole → 0.7 s
- Ventricular systole → 0.3 s → 3 phases
- Ventricular diastole → 0.5 s → 5 phases
- Systole → pumping / ejection
- Diastole → filling
### Clinical aspect
- Exercise → ↑HR → ↓ cardiac cycle (0.3 s)
  - Ventricular systole → 0.12 s
  - Ventricular diastole → 0.18 s
  - → ventricular filling compromised
- Cardiac cycle = 60 / HR → CC ∝ 1/HR

## Phases of cardiac cycle
### Ventricular systole
- 1. Isovolumetric ventricular contraction
  - Mitral valve closes → S1 heard
  - Aortic valve already closed
  - Volume same, pressure ↑↑
  - End of phase → aortic valve opens
- 2. Rapid ejection phase
  - 2/3rd of ventricular volume
  - Aortic valve opened
- 3. Slow ejection phase
  - Around 1/3rd ejected
  - End systolic volume = 50 mL

### Ventricular diastole
- 1. Protodiastole
  - Mitral valve closed
  - Aortic valve closes → S2 heard
- 2. Isovolumetric relaxation
  - Volume same, pressure ↓
- 3. Rapid filling phase
  - Mitral valve opens
  - Passive (with slow filling) → 80 %
- 4. Slow filling phase
  - AKA diastasis
- 5. Filling d/t atrial contraction
  - 20 % active filling → atrial kick
  - End diastolic volume = 120 mL

## Heart sounds
- S1 → closure of mitral & tricuspid → physiological
- S2 → closure of aortic & pulmonary → physiological
- S3 → vibration of ventricular wall in rapid filling
  - Physiological → children, after exercise
- S4 → stiff ventricles → forceful atrial contraction (> 20 %)
  - Pathological → hypertension, restrictive cardiomyopathy

## Pressure volume changes (PVC)
### Normal pressure–volume graph
- A → mitral valve opens (ESV 50)
- A → B → filling: RF, SF, AC
- B → mitral valve closes → S1 (EDV 120)
- B → C → IVC (isovolumetric contraction)
- C → aortic valve opens
- C → D → E → RE, then SE
- E → aortic valve closes → S2; PD
- E → A → IVR (isovolumetric relaxation)
- Width of loop → stroke volume

### Formulas
- SV = EDV − ESV = 120 − 50 = 70 mL
- EF = SV / EDV × 100 = 70/120 × 100
  - Normal EF = 55–60 %
- EDV = ESV / (1 − EF)
- ESV = EDV (1 − EF)

### Cardiac pathologies
- Mitral stenosis
  - Left shift
  - EDV ↓, ESV normal
  - ↓ stroke volume
- Aortic stenosis
  - EDV normal, ESV ↑↑
  - ↑ left ventricular pressure
- Aortic regurgitation
  - EDV ↑↑, ESV normal
  - Isovolumetric phase not straight
  - Height ↑, width ↑
- Mitral regurgitation
  - EDV ↑↑, ESV –
  - Isovolumetric phase not straight
  - Height ↓, width ↑

## Wiggers diagram
### Traces
- Aortic pressure
- Atrial pressure → a, c, v waves
- Ventricular pressure
- Ventricular volume → 130 to 50 mL
- Electrocardiogram
- Phonocardiogram → technique of recording heart sounds
### Phases on top
- Isovolumic contraction → ejection (RE & SE)
- Isovolumic relaxation → rapid inflow → diastasis → atrial systole
### Heart sounds on the time line
- S1 → R wave
- S2 → end of T wave
- S3 → rapid inflow
- S4 → P wave (forceful atrial contraction)
### Aortic pressure waveform
- Systolic phase → dicrotic notch → diastolic phase
- Dicrotic notch → closure of aortic valve → S2
### Terminologies
- Total electromechanical systole (Q–S2)
  - Onset of QRS → closure of aortic valve (S2)
- LVET
  - Carotid pressure rise → dicrotic notch
  - Correlates with ejection fraction
- Pre-ejection period (PEP)
  - PEP = QS2 − LVET
  - PEP / LVET = 0.35
  - ↑ ratio ∝ 1 / ejection fraction
