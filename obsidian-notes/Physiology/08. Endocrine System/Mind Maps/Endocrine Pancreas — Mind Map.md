# Endocrine Pancreas

## Islets of Langerhans — Cell Types
### Cells and hormones
- α cells → glucagon → ↑ blood glucose
- β cells (majority, 55 %)
  - Insulin → ↓ blood glucose
  - Amylin → potentiates insulin
    - Drug form → pramlintide (Rx DM)
  - C peptide → endogenous insulin marker
    - C peptide assay → estimates insulin release
    - C peptide : insulin = 1 : 1 (equimolar)
- δ cells → somatostatin (universal inhibitor)
- F cells (least %) → pancreatic polypeptide

### Gland
- Islet of Langerhans = endocrine pancreas
  - Abundant in tail of pancreas
- Acinar cells → digestive enzymes (exocrine)

## Insulin
### Properties
- Large protein hormone
- 51 amino acids
  - Chain A 21, chain B 30, disulphide bridges
- First protein
  - Completely sequenced (Sanger)
  - Produced by recombinant DNA technology
- Zinc stabilizes structure
- HNF mutation → MODY

### Release
- Food → ↑ glucose → insulin → ↓ glucose
  - Negative feedback system

### β cell mechanism (RMP −90 mV)
- Step 1 → glucose enters via GLUT-2 (glucose sensor)
  - Glucokinase → glucose 6 phosphate → pyruvate → ATP
- Step 2 → ATP closes ATP-sensitive K⁺ channel (SUR)
  - K⁺ ↑↑↑ inside → depolarisation
- Step 3 → voltage gated Ca²⁺ channel opens → Ca²⁺ in
- Step 4 → SNARE proteins → exocytosis
  - Insulin, amylin, C peptide

### Drugs on K-ATP channel
- Opened → ↓ insulin
  - Rx of insulinoma → diazoxide
- Closed → ↑ insulin
  - Rx of DM → sulfonylureas (secretagogues)

### Mutations
- Glucokinase → MODY 2
- GLUT-2 → Fanconi–Bickel syndrome
  - Defect in glucose sensing → ↓ insulin → hyperglycemia

### Transport
- Insulin dependent sites → muscle, adipose, heart
- Facilitated diffusion via GLUT-4
- Exercise → glucose into muscle independent of insulin
  - Exercise → AMP → GLUT-4 insertion
  - AMP-activated kinase pathway
  - Exercise important in Rx of DM

### Insulin receptor
- α α outside, β β span membrane
- Tyrosine kinase → insulin-receptor substrates (IRS)
- PI-3-kinase pathway
  - GLUT-4 insertion
  - Metabolic actions
- MAPK pathway
  - Promotion of growth

### Actions
- Rapid (seconds)
  - ↓ blood glucose (via GLUT-4)
  - ↓ K⁺ → Rx hyperkalemia (insulin + dextrose)
- Long term (days)
  - Major anabolic hormone → storage
- Stimulated
  - Glycogenesis
  - Lipogenesis
  - Glycolysis
- Inhibited
  - Glycogenolysis
  - Neoglucogenesis
  - Lipolysis
  - Ketogenesis

### Factors ↑ insulin secretion
- High blood glucose
- α cells → glucagon
- Amino acids → arginine, leucine
- Parasympathetic → vagal stimulation, ACh
- Sympathetic → only β receptor stimulation
- Gastrin, CCK, secretin
- Incretins
- Protein rich meal → also ↑ glucagon (prevents hypoglycemia)

### Factors ↓ insulin secretion
- Somatostatin
- Streptozocin, alloxan → destroy β cells (research only)
- Sympathetic → α action (dominant)

### Incretins — applied
- GLP-1 and GIP
- Degraded by DPP-4
- Oral glucose → incretins → ↑ insulin vs IV glucose
  - = incretin effect
- Management of diabetes
  - GLP-1 analogue → exenatide
  - DPP-4 blockers → gliptins (sitagliptin)
    - ↓ degradation → prolonged GLP-1 action

### Glucose homeostasis
- ↓ glucose → hypoglycemic hormone → insulin
  - Glucose < 30 mg/dl → coma
- ↑ glucose → counter regulatory hormones
  - Growth hormone → dawn phenomenon
  - Glucagon
  - T3
  - Cortisol
  - Epinephrine
