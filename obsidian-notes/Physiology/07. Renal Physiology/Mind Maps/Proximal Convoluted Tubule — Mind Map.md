# Proximal Convoluted Tubule

## Structure & Physiology
### Parts of nephron
- Nephron = functional unit of kidney
- Glomerulus → filtration
- Proximal convoluted tubule (PCT)
- Loop of Henle (LOH)
- Distal convoluted tubule (DCT)
- Collecting duct (CD)
- Basic processes
  - Filtration → reabsorption → secretion → excretion

### Structure of proximal tubule
- Microvilli = finger-like projections
  - ↑ surface area → ↑ reabsorption
- Mitochondria → ATP / energy for active transport
- Highly infolded plasma membrane
- Leaky tight junctions

### Segments of proximal tubule
- S1 → 1st half of PCT
- S2 → 2nd half of PCT + 1st half of straight tubule
- S3 → 2nd half of proximal straight tubule
  - More prone to ischemia (eg: shock)

## Reabsorption
### Proportion reabsorbed
- NaCl → 67 % (2/3rd)
- H₂O → 67 %
- K⁺ → 67 %
- Urea → 67 % (page value)
- Ca²⁺ → 70 %
- Phosphate & bicarbonate → 80 %
- Glucose, amino acids → 100 %
- Equal Na⁺ and H₂O → PCT fluid isotonic
- PCT = only segment absorbing glucose

### Sodium reabsorption
- Blood side → Na⁺ K⁺ ATPase
- Lumen side carriers
  - Na⁺–H⁺ exchanger (NHE) → major channel protein
  - SGLT2 → Na⁺ glucose cotransporter 2
  - Na⁺ amino acid cotransporter
  - Na⁺ PO₄³⁻ cotransporter
- ↑ Cl⁻ in lumen
  - d/t preferential Na⁺ absorption in early PCT
  - Reabsorbed in later parts of PCT
- SGLT1 → found in GIT

### Glucose reabsorption
- SGLT2
  - 100 % reabsorption of glucose
  - Carrier protein → 2° active transport
  - Can get saturated
- Blockers → canagliflozin, empagliflozin, sotagliflozin
  - ↓ reabsorption, ↑ excretion of glucose
  - → used to manage diabetes mellitus
  - → ↑ bacterial growth → UTI (older people)

### Saturation of SGLT2
- Plasma glucose 200 mg/dL → 375 transported (Tm)
- → saturation of SGLT2 → excess glucose excreted
- Splay
  - Deviation in reabsorption curve
  - d/t heterogeneous nephrons → different TmG
- Titration curve
  - Filtered level at glomerulus → straight line
  - Reabsorption → plateau due to saturation (TmG 375)
  - Excretion = renal glycosuria → starts at 200 mg/dL

### Water reabsorption
- Na⁺–H⁺ exchanger + aquaporin 1 (AQP-1)
- Obligatory water reabsorption → osmosis
  - Na⁺ follows H₂O (page wording)
  - Not influenced by hormones
- Facultative water reabsorption → collecting duct
  - Facilitated diffusion
  - Hormone dependent → ADH

### Tubular fluid / plasma ratio (TF/P)
- ↑ reabsorption → ↑ plasma conc, ↓ tubular conc
- TF/P = 1 → reabsorption same as water
- TF/P > 1 → Cl⁻, creatinine
  - Reabsorption < water
- TF/P = 1 → urea, Na⁺, Osm
- TF/P < 1 → HCO₃⁻, amino acids, glucose
  - Reabsorption > water

## Secretion
- PCT secretes PAH via carrier protein
  - → saturation seen
- Excretion of PAH = filtered load + secretion
- PAH curve
  - Filtered load → straight line
  - Secretion → plateau d/t saturation
  - Early excretion → more steep → E = F + S
  - Late excretion → less steep → E = F
