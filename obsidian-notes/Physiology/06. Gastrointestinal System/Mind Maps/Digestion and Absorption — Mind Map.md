# Digestion and Absorption

## Carbohydrates
### Digestion — diet polysaccharides
- Mouth → salivary amylase
  - Activated by chloride ions
  - Inhibited by gastric acid
- Stomach → no enzymes for carbohydrate
- Pancreas → pancreatic amylase
  - Major enzyme
  - α 1:4 linkages → disaccharides
- Small intestine → disaccharidases (brush border)
  - Lactase, isomaltase, sucrase
  - α 1:6 linkages → monosaccharides (glucose, fructose)

### Absorption
- Fructose → GLUT-5 (lumen side and blood side on the page)
- Glucose + Na⁺ → SGLT-1
  - Found in GIT
  - Secondary active transport
- Glucose out → GLUT-2 (facilitated diffusion)
- Clinical aspect
  - ORS → glucose facilitates Na⁺ absorption via SGLT-1
  - CGGM → congenital absence of SGLT-1
  - CGGM → autosomal recessive
  - CGGM → chronic refractory diarrhoea
  - SGLT-2 → kidney

## Lipids
### Digestion — diet triglycerides
- Mouth → lingual lipase
- Stomach → gastric lipase
- Pancreas → pancreatic lipase, colipase, cholesterol esterase
  - Act on TG → free fatty acids

### Absorption
- Bile-salt micelles (high concentration)
  - Free fatty acids, cholesterol
  - Fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E, K
- → passive diffusion → mucosal cell (low concentration)
  - FFA → re-esterification → triglycerides
  - TG → transported by chylomicrons
- Emulsification
  - Mechanism → bile salt + fat
  - Prevents large fat globules
  - ↑ lipase action → digestion & absorption of lipids

### Enterohepatic circulation
- Liver ⇄ terminal ileum
- 6–8 times/day
- Fewer new bile salts → conserves energy

### Short-chain fatty acids
- Propionate, butyrate, acetate
- Formed by colonic flora
- Trophic → ↑ mucosal growth
- Acid–base → absorbed in exchange for H⁺
- Prevent inflammation

## Protein
### Digestion — diet polypeptides
- Mouth → no enzymes
- Stomach → pepsin
- Pancreas → trypsin, chymotrypsin, carboxypeptidase
  - Zymogens → prevent autodigestion of pancreas
  - Activated in small intestine
  - Form dipeptides & tripeptides
- Endopeptidases → inner peptide bonds
  - Trypsin, chymotrypsin
- Exopeptidases → end peptide bonds
  - Aminopeptidase (H₂N end)
  - Carboxypeptidase (COOH end)

### Absorption
- Di/tripeptides + H⁺ → peptide transporter (PepT)
- Na⁺–H⁺ exchange → recycles H⁺
- Cytoplasmic protease → amino acids
- Basolateral amino-acid transporter → blood

## Vitamins, water absorption
### Vitamin absorption
- Fat-soluble → with micelles
- Water-soluble (B complex) → Na⁺-dependent cotransporters
- Except B12 & folate → Na⁺-independent

### Water absorption
- Total in GI tract 9 L
- Reabsorbed 8.8 L
- Stool 200 mL
- Maximum absorbed in jejunum

## Iron absorption
### Mechanism
- Site → duodenum
- Heme iron → heme transporter
- Non-heme Fe³⁺ → duodenal cytochrome B → Fe²⁺
- Fe²⁺ → DMT-1 → enterocyte
- Mucosal ferritin → lost by shedding of epithelial cells
- Ferroportin 1 → Fe²⁺ out
- Hephaestin → Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺
- Plasma transferrin → erythroid marrow
- Depends on
  - Dietary iron intake
  - Body iron stores
  - Erythropoietic activity (bone marrow)
- Factors ↑ → vitamin C
- Factors ↓ → tannins, oxalates, phytates

### Regulation
- Hepcidin → negative regulator from liver
- HFE gene mutation → haemochromatosis
- Represses basolateral iron transport
  - Binds ferroportin
  - Iron not released from macrophages & other cells
- Links body iron stores with absorption
