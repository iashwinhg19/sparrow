# Calcium Homeostasis

## Distribution & absorption of Ca²⁺
- Normal calcium 8.7–10.2 mg/dL
### Distribution of total calcium
- Free form (50 %)
  - Ionic calcium
  - Most active → responsible for actions
  - Actions → muscle contraction, transmitter & hormone release
  - Actions → exocrine secretion, enzyme activation
- Bound form (41 %)
  - Bound to albumin
  - Storage form, not active
- Complex form (9 %)
  - Citrate
  - Phosphate
### Hypocalcemic tetany
- Free : bound ratio set by
  - Blood pH
  - Albumin (binds both H⁺ and Ca²⁺)
- Mechanism
  - ↓ Ca²⁺ → Na⁺ channels open more frequently
  - → ↑ depolarisation → ↑ action potential frequency
  - → hyperexcitability → tetany
- Signs (d/t hyperexcitability)
  - Trousseau's → cuff 20 mmHg above SBP × 3 min → carpopedal spasm
  - Chvostek's → tap facial nerve at angle of jaw → facial twitching
- Respiratory alkalosis → ↓ H⁺ → ↑ Ca²⁺–albumin binding → ↓ free Ca²⁺
### Absorption of Ca²⁺
- Site → duodenum & proximal jejunum
- ↑ absorption → gastric acid, protein-rich diet
- ↓ absorption → phytates, oxalates, fatty acids
  - Form insoluble Ca²⁺ complexes

## Hormones regulating Ca²⁺ homeostasis
### Three hormone three organ model
- Hormones
  - Parathormone → ↑ Ca²⁺
  - Vitamin D → ↑ Ca²⁺
  - Calcitonin → ↓ Ca²⁺
- Organs
  - Bone → bank for Ca²⁺
  - GIT → absorption of dietary Ca²⁺
  - Kidneys
### Parathormone (PTH)
- Site → parathyroid gland
  - Principle / chief cells → secrete PTH
  - Oxyphil cells
- Structure → 84 amino acids
- Bone
  - PTH receptor on osteoblasts → RANKL
  - RANKL → osteoclast differentiation → enzymes
  - → dissolves hydroxyapatite → releases Ca²⁺ & PO₄³⁻
  - Denosumab ⊣ RANKL → osteoporosis Rx
  - Osteoblast = formation, osteoclast = resorption
- Teriparatide = drug form of PTH (34 AA)
  - Continuous → bone resorption
  - Intermittent → bone formation
- Kidney
  - PCT → ⊣ Na⁺–PO₄³⁻ cotransporter → phosphaturia
  - PTH AKA phosphatonin
  - DCT → ↑ Ca²⁺ reabsorption
- GIT → indirect → ↑ vitamin D → ↑↑ Ca²⁺ absorption
- Overall → ↑ S.Ca²⁺, ↓ S.PO₄³⁻
- CaSR on chief cell
  - High Ca²⁺ ⊖, low Ca²⁺ ⊕ → PTH release
  - Mutation → PTH not suppressed → FHH
### Vitamin D
- Synthesis
  - 7-dehydrocholesterol (skin) → UV-B 280–320 nm
  - → vitamin D3 / cholecalciferol
  - → 25-hydroxylase (liver) → calcidiol
  - Calcidiol → lab test for vitamin D status
  - → 1α-hydroxylase (kidney) = rate limiting step
  - → calcitriol = most active form
- GIT → Ca²⁺ absorption from duodenum
  - ↑ calbindin, ↑ Ca²⁺ ATPase, ↑ alkaline phosphatase
- Bone → dual action
  - Low Ca²⁺ → resorption (VDR on osteoblast, like PTH)
  - High Ca²⁺ → formation
- Kidney → ↑ reabsorption of Ca²⁺ & PO₄³⁻
- Overall → ↑ S.Ca²⁺, ↑ S.PO₄³⁻
### Calcitonin
- Source → parafollicular C cells of thyroid
- Tumour marker → medullary carcinoma thyroid
- Calcium lowering hormone
  - Bone → receptor on osteoclast → ⊣ activity → ↓ resorption
  - GIT → ↓ Ca²⁺ absorption
  - Kidney → ↓ Ca²⁺ reabsorption
- Pregnancy & lactation
  - ↑ Ca²⁺ demand → bone reserve used
  - Calcitonin prevents excessive bone loss
- Analogue → salmon fish → salcatonin
### Paraneoplastic hypercalcemia of malignancy
- D/t PTHrP
- Squamous cell carcinoma of lung
- Small cell carcinoma of lung

## Bone markers
### Formation markers
- Osteocalcin
- Alkaline phosphatase
- Pro-peptides of type I collagen
### Resorption markers
- Blood → TRAP (tartrate resistant acid phosphatase)
- Blood → MMP (matrix metalloproteinase)
- Blood → cathepsin K
- Urine → NTX (N telopeptides of collagen crosslinks)
- Urine → CTX (C telopeptides of collagen crosslinks)

## Summary
### PTH
- Stimulus → ↓ serum Ca²⁺
- Bone → ↑ resorption
- Intestine → indirect via ↑ vitamin D
- Kidney → ↑ Ca²⁺ reabsorption, ↑ PO₄³⁻ excretion
- Overall → ↑ S.Ca²⁺, ↓ S.PO₄³⁻
### Vitamin D
- Stimulus → ↓ Ca²⁺, ↓ PO₄³⁻, ↑ PTH
- Bone → both formation & resorption
- Intestine → ↑ Ca²⁺ & PO₄³⁻ absorption
- Kidney → ↑ Ca²⁺ & PO₄³⁻ reabsorption
- Overall → ↑ S.Ca²⁺, ↑ S.PO₄³⁻
### Calcitonin
- Stimulus → ↑ serum Ca²⁺
- Bone → inhibits resorption
- Intestine → ↓ Ca²⁺ absorption
- Kidney → ↑ Ca²⁺ excretion
- Overall → ↓ S.Ca²⁺
