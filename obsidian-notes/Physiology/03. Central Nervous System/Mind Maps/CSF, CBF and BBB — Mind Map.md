# CSF, CBF and BBB

## Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)
### General properties
- Function → lubrication & cushion
- Site of production → ependymal cells of choroid plexus
- Ependymal cell
  - Glial cell
  - Lines choroid plexus
  - Secretes CSF
- Blood–CSF barrier → fenestrated capillary, ependymal layer
### Parameters
- Volume → 150 ml
- Rate of production → 550 ml/day
- Turnover → 3.7 times/day
- Pressure → 70–180 mm H₂O
  - Average → 112 mm H₂O (filtration = absorption)
### Regulation
- At the level of absorption
- ↓ absorption → ↑ CSF volume → ↑ CSF pressure
### Circulation
- 1 Lateral ventricles
- → foramen of Monro → 2 III ventricle (interthalamic adhesion)
- → aqueduct of Sylvius → 3 IV ventricle
- → foramina of Luschka & foramen of Magendie
- → 4 subarachnoid space → 5 arachnoid villi
- → 6 venous sinuses → absorb CSF
### CSF vs plasma
- Higher in CSF → Cl⁻, Mg²⁺
- Equal → osmolality, bicarbonate
- Lower in CSF → glucose (2/3 of plasma), proteins
- ↑ protein in CSF → infection
### Applied aspect
- CSF leak
  - Marker → β₂-transferrin (only in CSF)
  - Rhinorrhoea → leak through nose
  - Otorrhoea → leak through ears
- Post lumbar puncture headache
  - LP → CSF loss
  - → cushioning lost → traction on brain
  - → headache

## Cerebral blood flow (CBF)
### General properties
- Blood supply → circle of Willis
- Grey matter > white matter
- Function → nutrition
- Normal CBF → 750 ml/min = 14 % of CO
- O₂ use → 20 % of resting body O₂ → hypoxia-sensitive
- Most hypoxia-sensitive areas
  - Hippocampus
  - Thalamus
  - Inferior colliculus
  - Basal ganglia
- Liver → highest % of cardiac output
### Factors affecting CBF
- Autoregulation → 65–140 mmHg → CBF constant
- Hypercarbia → ↑ pCO₂ → ↑ CBF
- Hypothermia → ↓ 1 °C → ↓ CBF by 7 %
  - Therapeutic hypothermia in neurosurgery

## Blood brain barrier (BBB)
### Structure
- Endothelial cells → tight junctions
- Astrocytes → star-shaped glial cells
  - Foot processes around capillaries
### Can cross
- Lipid-soluble → steroid hormones
- Gases → O₂, CO₂, nitrogen
- Glucose → GLUT-1 transporter
- Water → aquaporin-4 channels
### Cannot cross
- Water-soluble → protein hormones
- Ions → H⁺
- Neurotransmitters → dopamine
### Applied aspect
- Caisson's disease → ↑ N₂ in brain
- Neuromyelitis optica (Devic's)
  - Autoantibodies to aquaporin-4 → demyelination
- L-DOPA crosses BBB → Parkinson's treatment
### Gatekeeper
- P-glycoprotein → filters drugs crossing BBB
- P-gp blockers → concentrate drug in brain
  - Used in brain neoplasms
### Circumventricular organs
- BBB absent
- Mnemonic SOAP
  - Subfornical organ (SFO)
  - Organum vasculosum of lamina terminalis (OVLT)
  - Area postrema (AP)
  - Posterior pituitary
- Allow passage of protein hormones
- Angiotensin II → SFO → ↑ thirst
- Angiotensin II → AP → ↑ BP
### Monro–Kellie doctrine
- CSF + blood + brain volume = constant
- Rise in one → compresses the other two
- ↑ CSF → ↓ blood flow
- ↑ CSF → compresses brain tissue
