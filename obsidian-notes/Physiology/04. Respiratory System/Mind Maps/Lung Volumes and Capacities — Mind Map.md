# Lung Volumes and Capacities

## Time independent vs time dependent
- Time independent
  - AKA static volumes
  - Eg → tidal volume
- Time dependent
  - AKA dynamic volumes
  - Eg → FEV1 (forced expiratory volume in 1st second)

## Static Lung Volume & Capacities
### Spirogram
- Normal breathing → between 2500 and 3000 mL
- Maximum inspiration → 6000 mL
- Maximum expiration → 1200 mL
### Volumes
- Inspiratory reserve volume (IRV) → 2–3 L
- Tidal volume (TV) → 500 ml
- Expiratory reserve volume (ERV) → 1.3 L
- Residual volume (RV) → 1.2 L
### Capacities
- Inspiratory capacity (IC) = IRV + TV
- Functional residual capacity (FRC)
  - AKA equilibrium volume
  - = TLC/2 or ERV + RV → 2.5 L
- Vital capacity = IRV + TV + ERV
- Total lung capacity = IRV + TV + ERV + RV → 5 L
### Closing volume
- Volume in lower dependent alveoli at end of expiration
- Closing capacity = closing volume + residual volume
### Investigations
- Spirometry
  - Measures all lung volumes except RV
  - → cannot calculate FRC & TLC
- Body plethysmography
  - Use → to measure RV
  - Principle → Boyle's law P ∝ 1/V
- Interpretation in inspiration
  - Lungs → volume ↑, pressure ↓
  - Chamber → volume ↓, pressure ↑
- Formula
  - Pm i × VL i = Pm f × (VL i + ΔV)
  - Pm i → initial mouth pressure = atmospheric = 760 mm Hg
  - VL i → initial lung volume = FRC
  - Pm f → final pressure at mouth
  - ΔV → change in volume
- Worked example
  - Mouth pressure ↓ by 10 mm Hg, gas expands by 50 ml
  - 760 × FRC = 750 × (FRC + 50)
  - 10 FRC = 37500 → FRC = 3750 ml
- Obsolete methods for RV (page)
  - Helium dilution technique
  - Nitrogen washout technique

## Dynamic Lung Volume & Capacities
### Forced vital capacity (FVC)
- Deep inspiration
- → followed by fast & forceful expiration in 6 sec
### FEV1
- 70–80 % of air expired in first second (healthy)
- Tiffeneau–Pinelli index → FEV1/FVC = 70–80 %
- Use → obstructive vs restrictive lung disease
  - Obstructive → difficulty in expiration
  - Restrictive → difficulty in inspiration
### Patterns
- Obstructive (emphysema)
  - FEV1 ↓ → ratio ↓↓
- Restrictive early stage
  - FEV1 ↓, FVC ↓ → ratio normal
- Restrictive late stage
  - FEV1 ↓, FVC ↓↓ → ratio ↑↑
- GOLD criteria for COPD → based on FEV1/FVC

## Flow Volume Loop
### Normal lung
- Expiratory limb above, inspiratory limb below
- PEFR → peak expiratory flow rate
- Area under curve = FVC
- FVC = TLC − RV
- Width → VC
- Trachea → early rising limb
- Bronchi (medium & small airway) → most sensitive to obstruction
### Obstructive vs restrictive
- Obstructive → scooped out / dog leg pattern
- Restrictive → miniature version of normal loop
### Airway obstruction
- Intrathoracic (foreign body obstruction)
  - Variable intrathoracic (LAO) → expiratory limb flattened
  - Fixed upper airway obstruction (UAO)
- Extrathoracic (sleep apnoea)
  - Fixed upper airway obstruction (UAO)
  - Variable extrathoracic (UAO) → inspiratory limb flattened
- Fixed UAO → both limbs flattened
### Expiratory flow volume curve
- Normal → peak about 400 L/min, TLC to RV
- Airway obstruction → starts at large TLC, low peak, high RV
- Restrictive lung disease → small TLC, low RV
- TLC → total lung capacity
- RV → residual volume
