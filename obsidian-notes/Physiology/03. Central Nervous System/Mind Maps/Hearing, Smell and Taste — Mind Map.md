# Hearing, Smell and Taste

## Hearing
### Cochlea
- Vestibulocochlear nerve (8th cranial nerve)
  - Vestibular part → maintaining balance
  - Cochlear part → hearing
- Scala vestibuli & scala tympani → perilymph (rich in Na⁺)
- Reissner's membrane → roof of scala media
- Scala media → lined with stria vascularis
  - → secretes K⁺-rich endolymph
  - Endolymphatic potential → +80 mV
- Spiral ganglion → cochlear branch of N VIII

### Organ of Corti
- Senses sound
- Relays information to spiral ganglion
- Inner & outer hair cells under tectorial membrane

### Hair cells
- Deiter cell → supporting cell
- Cilia
  - Tallest → kinocilium
  - Shorter → stereocilia
- Outer hair cells → more in number, less innervated
- Inner hair cells → less in number, more innervated
- Towards kinocilium
  - → K⁺ influx from endolymph
  - → depolarization → conducts nerve impulse
- Away from kinocilium
  - → hyperpolarization → inhibits nerve conduction

### Basilar membrane
- AKA frequency analyzer
- Principle → travelling wave theory
- Base
  - Broad (page) — textbooks: narrow, stiff
  - Close to oval window
  - ↑ vibration by high frequency waves
- Apex
  - Narrow (page) — textbooks: wide, floppy
  - Close to helicotrema
  - ↑ vibration by low frequency waves

### Applied aspect
- Hair cells prone to damage → ototoxicity
  - Outer > inner
  - Commonly drugs → aminoglycosides
- Otoacoustic emissions (OAE)
  - K⁺ current in OHCs → Kemp waves
  - Sensitive hearing test

### Auditory pathway
- Mnemonic → E COLI MA
- Eighth nerve
- Cochlear nuclei
- Superior olivary nucleus
- Lateral lemniscus
- Inferior colliculus
- Medial geniculate body
- Auditory cortex
- Direction of sound source → superior olive & inferior colliculus
- Auditory cortex
  - Higher order processing centre for hearing
  - Brodmann 41, 42
  - Superior temporal / Heschl's gyrus / planum temporale

### Stapedial reflex
- AKA attenuation reflex / preventive reflex
- Loud noise → 8th nerve (afferent)
- → superior olivary nucleus (centre)
- → facial nerve (efferent) → stapedius contracts
- → attenuates sound → prevents tympanic membrane rupture
- Facial nerve paralysis → stapedius fails → hyperacusis

## Smell
### Olfactory epithelium
- Roof of nasal cavity
- Sustentacular cells → supportive
- Olfactory neurons → bipolar
- Bowman's gland → secretes mucus
- Basal cell → stem cell for regeneration
- Cribriform plate fracture → damages olfactory neurons
### Olfactory bulb
- Mitral & tufted cells
  - NT glutamate → excitatory
- Granule & periglomerular cells
  - NT GABA → inhibitory
### Olfaction
- Olfactory bulb → thalamus → dorsomedian nucleus
- Olfactory bulb → limbic cortex (smell brain)
  - Entorhinal cortex → smell with memory
  - Amygdala → smell with emotions
- Olfactory cortex (orbitofrontal) → conscious perception of smell
- Entorhinal cortex → earliest degeneration in Alzheimer's

## Taste (gustation)
### Taste buds — receptors
- Sweet → T1R (G-protein coupled)
- Salt → epithelial Na channels (ENaC)
- Sour → HCN channel
- Bitter → T2 receptors
  - Helps survival → most toxins bitter
- Umami → mGluR4
  - Highly addictive → ripe tomatoes
### Cells
- Type I → glial cells
- Type II → taste receptor cells
- Type III → presynaptic cell
- Type IV → basal (stem) cell
### Taste pathway
- Ant 2/3 tongue → chorda tympani N.
- Post 1/3 tongue → glossopharyngeal N.
- Palate & pharynx → vagus N.
- → nucleus tractus solitarius (medulla) → integration centre
- → ventral posteromedial nucleus of thalamus
- → taste centre → anterior nucleus, frontal operculum
