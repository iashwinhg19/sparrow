# Cellular Physiology

## The eukaryotic human cell
- Cell = fundamental unit of all living organisms
- Eukaryotic → true nucleus + membrane-bound organelles
- Labelled parts
  - Plasma membrane, cytoplasm
  - Nucleus, nucleolus, chromatin
  - RER, SER, ribosomes
  - Golgi apparatus, Golgi vesicle, secretory vesicle
  - Mitochondria, lysosome, peroxisome, vacuole
  - Centrosome, microtubule, microfilament, intermediate filament

## Cell membrane
- AKA plasma membrane
- Function → outer barrier with selective permeability
- Structure → fluid mosaic model
  - Proposed by Singer and Nicolson
- Composition (macromolecules)
  - Proteins 55 % → mosaic pattern
  - Lipids 40 % → fluidity
  - Carbohydrates 5 %

## Lipids
### Introduction
- Structure
  - Bilayer → outer and inner lipids
  - Asymmetry → each lipid on a specific side
- Types
  - Phospholipids (PO₄³⁻ group)
  - Glycolipids (carbohydrate side chain)
  - Cholesterol (critical lipid)

### Phospholipids (major lipid)
- Phosphatidylcholine (PC) = lecithin
  - Lungs → surfactant
  - Prevents alveolar collapse
  - Major lipid in surfactant
- Sphingomyelin
  - Nerve cell membrane, surfactant
  - Minor lipid in surfactant
- Phosphatidylinositol (IP3)
  - Endocrine cells
  - Second messenger → IP3 → DAG → Ca²⁺
- Phosphatidylserine (PS)
  - Inner surface of membrane in all cells
  - On outer surface → "eat me" signal
  - → apoptosis (programmed cell death)
  - Detected by Annexin V (binds PS) → Annexin V staining
- Cardiolipin (CL)
  - Heart → mitochondria
  - Syphilis → serum anti-CL antibody → VDRL test
- L/S ratio (lecithin / sphingomyelin)
  - Assesses fetal lung maturity
  - Mature lung ≥ 2

### Glycolipids
- Cerebrosides → CNS
- Gangliosides → GIT
  - GM-1 → receptor for cholera toxin fragment B

### Cholesterol
- Site → cell membrane of skin
- Precursor of vitamin D
  - 7-dehydrocholesterol → sunlight (skin) → vit D
- Fluidity buffer → keeps fluidity in optimal range

### Membrane fluidity
- Lubricative property of lipid → fluid membrane → allows mobility
- Increased fluidity
  - Good for health
  - Unsaturated (essential) fatty acids → kinked tails
  - Linoleic, linolenic, arachidonic acid
  - Omega-3 fatty acids → abundant in fish
- Decreased fluidity
  - Bad for health
  - Saturated (trans) fatty acids → straight, rigid
  - Stearic acid
  - Palmitic acid → abundant in junk food

## Proteins
### Classification by location
- Transmembrane / integral → present throughout
- Peripheral → present peripherally
- Lipid-anchored → need lipid for attachment

### Transmembrane proteins
- Most important
- Hormone receptors (m/c) → GPCR
- Pump channels → Na⁺–K⁺ ATPase
- Ion channels → Cl⁻ channel (CFTR)
  - Cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator
  - Cystic fibrosis → CFTR mutation

### Peripheral proteins
- Support shape of cell
  - RBC (biconcave disc)
    - Spectrin → elliptocytosis
    - Ankyrin → spherocytosis
  - Skeletal muscle → dystrophin
    - Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)
    - Muscular weakness → typical presentation
    - Gowers' sign → climbs own body: knee → hip → stand
    - Respiratory paralysis (diaphragm weak) → death
- Cell surface receptors

### Lipid-anchored proteins (RBC)
- PIGA gene (phosphatidylinositol glycan-A) makes
  - CD55 → decay accelerating factor
  - CD59 → membrane inhibitor of reactive lysis
  - C8 binding protein
- Bind to lipid → phosphatidylinositol
- Normal gene → prevents hemolysis
- Mutated gene → PNH

### Paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria
- Pathology → mutated or absent PIGA gene
- Sleep (night) → ↓ pH
  - → complement C5–C9 (membrane attack complex)
  - → attacks RBC membrane
  - → hemolysis (hemoglobinuria)
- Treatment
  - Anti-C5 monoclonal antibody → supportive
    - Eculizumab
    - Ravulizumab (long acting)
  - Hematopoietic stem cell transplant → definitive

## Carbohydrates
### Classification
- Glycoprotein → attached to proteins
- Glycolipids → attached to lipids

### Significance
- Glycolipids on RBC membrane → determine blood groups
  - A, B, AB, O red cell types

### Notes
- Highest protein membrane → inner mitochondrial membrane
- Myelin → lipid 80 % : protein 20 %
- Damaged membrane repaired → resealing of lipid bilayer
- Lecithin deficiency in pre-term baby → hyaline membrane disease
