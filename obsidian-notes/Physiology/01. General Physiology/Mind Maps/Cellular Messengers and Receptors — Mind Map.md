# Cellular Messengers and Receptors

## Introduction
- Hormones act on cells by receptors
- Receptors can be
  - Extracellular
  - Intracellular

## Classification of Hormones & Hormone Receptors
### Classification of hormones
- Amino acid derivatives → cannot cross membrane → extracellular receptors
  - Tyrosine → epinephrine, norepinephrine, dopamine
    - = Catecholamines
  - Tyrosine → thyroid hormones (exception → nuclear receptor)
  - Tryptophan → serotonin, melatonin
  - Histidine → histamine
- Peptide & protein hormones → cannot cross → extracellular receptors
  - 3 AAs → TRH
  - 51 AAs → insulin
  - 84 AAs → parathormone
- Cholesterol derivatives → cross membrane → intracellular receptor
  - Steroids → estrogen, testosterone, aldosterone, cortisol
- Vitamins → lipid soluble → intracellular receptor
  - Vitamin A
  - Vitamin D

### Classification of hormone receptors
- Based on location
  - Extracellular → cell membrane receptors
    - Act via second messengers
  - Intracellular
    - Cytoplasmic receptors
    - Nuclear receptors

## Extracellular Receptors
- Types
  - G protein coupled receptors (GPCRs)
  - Tyrosine kinase (TK) receptor
  - Janus kinase receptor
  - Serine kinase receptor
- Hormone = 1st messenger
- Signal inside = 2nd messenger

### G-protein coupled receptor
- Transmembrane protein
- AKA serpentine / 7 transmembrane receptor
- Subunits
  - Alpha (α) → has GTPase activity
  - Beta (β)
  - Gamma (γ)
- Mechanism of action
  - Hormone binds GPCR
  - → α-subunit separates
  - → hydrolyses GTP to GDP
  - → energy liberated
  - → 2nd messengers sent inside cell
- 2nd messengers → activate protein kinases
  - Cyclic AMP
  - Ca²⁺ (IP3–DAG–Ca²⁺)
  - Cyclic GMP
- GPCR can use any 2nd messenger
  - Gs → stimulates adenylyl cyclase → ↑ cAMP
  - Gi → inhibits adenylyl cyclase → ↓ cAMP
  - Gq → ↑ Ca²⁺
  - Gt → transducin in retinal rods → PDE → ↓ cGMP

### Cyclic AMP effects
- Metabolism → glycogenolysis
  - Glucagon & epinephrine (1st messengers)
- Electrolyte secretion → secretin
  - → ↑ HCO3⁻ in pancreatic juice
  - Secretin = nature's antacid
- Water reabsorption → vasopressin (V2)
  - → ↑ aquaporin-2

### Clinical application of electrolyte secretion
- Cholera toxin → stimulates Gs
  - → ↑ adenylyl cyclase → ↑↑ cAMP
  - → ↑ electrolyte secretion → diarrhoea
  - → dehydration, death
- Pertussis toxin → inhibits Gi
  - → ↑↑ cAMP → electrolyte secretion in sputum
  - → whooping cough
- Gain of function of Gsα → ↑↑ cAMP
  - Precocious puberty
  - Skeletal malformations
  - Café au lait spots
  - = McCune Albright syndrome

### Calcium effects
- Uterine contraction → oxytocin
- Vasoconstriction → vasopressin (V1), angiotensin
- Exocytosis → TRH, cholecystokinin (CCK)
- cGMP = opposite of Ca²⁺
  - → relaxation & vasodilatation
  - Nitric oxide, atrial natriuretic peptide

### Tyrosine kinase receptor
- Insulin → TK receptor → ↑ cell growth & differentiation
- BCR-ABL translocation (Philadelphia chromosome)
  - → tyrosine kinase activated
  - → pathological growth & differentiation → CML
  - Rx → imatinib mesylate (blocks TK)

### Janus kinase receptor
- AKA cytokine receptor
- JAK-STAT
  - Signal transduction & activators of transcription
- Acted upon by
  - Growth hormone
  - Prolactin
  - Erythropoietin
  - Leptin

### Serine kinase receptor
- Reproductive functions
- Acted upon by
  - Activins
  - Anti-Müllerian hormone

## Intracellular Receptors
- Hormones that enter the cell
### Cytoplasmic receptors
- Inactive receptor held by heat shock proteins
- Hormone binds → hormone–receptor complex (active)
- → enters nucleus → binds DNA
- Mineralocorticoid (aldosterone)
- Glucocorticoid (cortisol)
- Androgen (testosterone)
- Vitamin D

### Nuclear receptor
- A/B → near N terminal, AF-1, activates transcription
- C → DNA binding domain
- D → hinge domain
- E → ligand binding domain (steroids)
- F → AF-2, activates transcription
- Thyroid hormone receptor
- Vitamin A receptor
- Estrogen receptor
- Progesterone receptor

## Homeostasis of Hormones & Neurotransmitter Receptors
### Safety mechanism for hormone overactivity
- Internalization of receptors
- Desensitization → receptors less responsive
- Inactivation of receptors

### Neurotransmitter receptors
- Ligand gated → ion channels → fast
  - Nicotinic ACh receptor
  - GABA A, GABA C
- GPCR / metabotropic → 2nd messengers → slow
  - Muscarinic ACh receptor
  - GABA B
