# Collecting Duct and Micturition Reflex

## Collecting Duct
- Hormones influencing CD
  - Aldosterone
  - Anti-diuretic hormone / vasopressin
- Parts
  - Cortical collecting duct (CCD)
  - Medullary collecting duct (MCD)

### Aldosterone
- Steroid hormone from adrenal cortex
- Can cross the cell membrane
- Hyperkalemia → major stimulus for release
- Site → principal cell
- Mechanism
  - Binds inactive mineralocorticoid receptor (MR)
  - → active MR–aldosterone complex → enters nucleus
  - ⊕ ENaC → Na⁺ & H₂O reabsorption
  - ⊕ Na⁺–K⁺ ATPase → K⁺ excretion via ROMK
- Spironolactone → blocks MR receptor

### Type A intercalated cell
- In acidosis
- CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃ → HCO₃⁻ + H⁺
- Lumen → H⁺ ATPase → H⁺ excretion
- Lumen → H⁺ K⁺ ATPase, upregulated by aldosterone
- Blood side → Cl⁻–HCO₃⁻ exchanger

### Applied aspect of aldosterone
- Amiloride → blocks ENaC
  - Diuretic → Na⁺ and H₂O excreted
- Spironolactone → blocks MR
  - K⁺ sparing diuretic
- Aldosterone inactive
  - Na⁺, H₂O excretion
  - K⁺ spared
- Liddle's syndrome → overactive ENaC
  - ↑↑ Na⁺ & H₂O reabsorption → ↑ body fluid volume
  - → hypertension

### ADH / vasopressin
- Site of action
  - P cells
  - Medullary collecting duct
- Major action → solute free water reabsorption
  - By facilitated diffusion
- ADH = protein hormone
  - Can't cross cell membrane
  - 1st messenger
- Mechanism
  - V2 (GPCR) → Gs → adenylyl cyclase
  - ATP → cAMP → protein kinase A
  - Protein phosphorylation → AQP-2 to luminal membrane
  - Hormone dependent facultative H₂O reabsorption
  - Basolateral → AQP-3, AQP-4
- Conivaptan → blocks V2

### Applied aspect of ADH
- Vaptans (conivaptan) → V2 receptor blockers
  - ↑ pure water excretion in urine
- Vasopressin in cancer → SIADH
  - Kidney escapes ↑↑ ADH → downregulates AQP-2

### Factors affecting ADH secretion
- ↑ ADH → water conservation
  - ↑ osmotic pressure
  - ↓ ECF
  - Painful stimuli
  - Nausea and vomiting
- ↓ ADH → water excretion
  - ↓ osmotic pressure
  - ↑ ECF volume
  - Alcohol (inhibits ADH)

### Vasopressin receptors
- V1 → blood vessel
  - Vasoconstriction → ↓ blood loss in vascular injury
  - Terlipressin → Rx of esophageal varices
- V2 → collecting duct, vascular endothelium
  - Release of vWF from endothelium
  - Desmopressin (intranasal) → von Willebrand disease
- V3 → anterior pituitary
  - ACTH release

### Free water clearance
- Negative
  - Urine concentration high, ADH high
  - Seen in SIADH
- Positive
  - Urine concentration low, ADH low / absent
  - Seen in diabetes insipidus
- Zero → kidney lost ability to dilute or concentrate
- Max water reabsorption → PCT (67 %)

## Natriuretic Peptides
### Types and site
- A type (ANP) → atrium
- B type (BNP) → cardiac ventricles
- C type (CNP) → vascular endothelium
- Site of action → terminal DCT and CD

### Actions
- Natriuresis → ↑ Na⁺ excretion
  - Afferent dilatation → ↑ GFR → ↑ Na⁺ filtration & excretion
- Regulation of ANP
  - Fluid overload, ↑ ECF → ↑ ANP
  - Hypovolemia, ↓ central (venous) pressure → ↓ ANP
- Physiological antagonism → homeostasis
  - ↑ Na⁺ → natriuretic peptide
  - ↓ Na⁺ → RAAS
- Aldosterone escape
  - Kidneys escape aldosterone in hyperaldosteronism
  - ↑↑ aldosterone → ↑ Na⁺ & H₂O → ↑ BP, no edema
  - ↑ BP → pressure diuresis
  - ANP (aldosterone antagonist) → natriuresis

## Micturition Reflex
- Ureter smooth muscle → pacemaker → peristalsis → bladder → micturition

### Neural control — spinal segments
- Sympathetic → L1–L3
  - Mediates pain signals from bladder
  - Prevents retrograde ejaculation
- Parasympathetic → S2–S4
  - Facilitates micturition
- Somatic → S2, S3, S4
  - Nucleus → Onuf's nucleus
  - Function → voluntary control

### Higher centres
- Facilitatory
  - Pons → Barrington centre
  - Posterior hypothalamus → cold → ↑ frequency
- Inhibitory → midbrain
- Centre for control of micturition
  - Site → paracentral lobule of cortex
  - Highest order of control
  - Blood supply → anterior cerebral artery
  - Lesion → urinary incontinence

### Reflex
- Bladder filling → stretch (stimulus)
- → afferents to spinal cord
- → parasympathetic activation
- → contraction of detrusor
- → relaxation of internal urethral sphincter
- → relaxation of external urethral sphincter → micturition
- Figure labels
  - Trigone of bladder
  - Internal urethral sphincter
  - Prostate, verumontanum, ejaculatory duct opening
  - Bulbourethral gland
  - External urethral sphincter

### Cystometrogram
- Study of volume and pressure changes in bladder
- Ia → filling starts → V ↑, P slight rise
- Ib → filling → V ↑↑↑, P constant
  - Laplace law → ↑ T, ↑ r → P stays constant
- 150 mL → 1st urge to void
- 400 mL → marked sense of fullness
- II → P ↑↑↑ → micturition occurs
