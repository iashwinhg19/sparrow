# Characteristics of Blood Vessels

## The vascular tree
- Heart → aorta → arteries → arterioles → capillaries → veins → heart
- Aorta → Windkessel (elastic store)
- Arterioles → resistance vessels
- Capillaries → exchange vessels
- Veins → capacitance vessels

## Aorta
### Elasticity / Windkessel effect
- Maintains diastolic blood pressure
- D/t elastin & fibrillin (supporting proteins)
- During systole → stretches → SBP = 120 mmHg
- During diastole → elastic recoil → DBP = 80 mmHg

### Applied aspect
- Mutation of elastin
  - → Williams syndrome
  - → supravalvular aortic stenosis
- Mutation of fibrillin
  - → Marfan syndrome
  - → dilatation of aortic root
  - → rupture → death

### Velocity & cross-sectional area
- Velocity of blood flow ∝ 1 / cross-sectional area
- Aorta
  - Max velocity → thickness & blood pressure
  - Lowest cross-sectional area

## Arterioles
- AKA resistance vessels
- Wall
  - Tunica externa
  - Tunica media → vascular smooth muscle (VSM)
  - Tunica intima
- Microcirculation
  - Arteriole → metarteriole → thoroughfare channel → venule
  - Precapillary sphincters guard each capillary
- Function → regulates blood flow
  - VSM contracts → vasoconstriction → ↑ resistance → ↓ blood flow
  - VSM relaxes → vasodilation → ↓ resistance → ↑ blood flow

## Capillaries & lymphatic system
### Capillaries
- AKA exchange vessels
- Function → exchange of nutrients & gases
- Characteristics
  - Volume of blood → 5 % of total
  - Flow → intermittent (vasomotion of precapillary sphincters)
  - Cross-sectional area → highest
  - Velocity → lowest (sluggish)

### Starling forces
- Capillary hydrostatic pressure (Pc) = 25 mmHg
- Interstitial oncotic pressure (πi) = 3 mmHg
- Capillary oncotic pressure (πc) = 25 mmHg
- Interstitial hydrostatic pressure (Pi) = −2 mmHg
- Net filtration pressure = (Pc + πi) − (πc + Pi)
- Arterial end → outward forces win → filtration
- Venous end → inward forces win → reabsorption
- Pi positive in skeletal muscle, liver & brain

### Lymphatic circulation
- Filtration ≫ reabsorption
- Lymph carries excess interstitial fluid → blood vessels
- Factors ↑ lymph flow
  - Lymphatic pump
  - Muscle contraction
  - Blood vessel pulsations
  - Massaging
  - ↑ Pc within physiological limits

### Applied aspect — edema
- Accumulation of fluid in interstitial space
- ↓ πc → ↓ plasma proteins
  - ↓ production → liver disease (cirrhosis)
  - ↑ excretion → nephrotic syndrome
- ↑ Pc beyond physiological limit
  - Chronic hypertension → lymphatic regulation fails
- Lymphatic obstruction → filariasis

## Veins
### Characteristics
- AKA capacitance vessels
- Volume of blood → 55 % of total (storage reservoirs)
- High distensibility / compliance
  - Compliance = ↑↑ ΔV / ↓↓ ΔP
  - → high volume, low pressure system

### Applied aspect
- Lying down → ↑ venous return
  - → stored in pulmonary veins
  - → pulmonary congestion
  - → breathlessness (orthopnoea)

### Note
- Vena cava → max diameter, min blood pressure
- Arteriovenous anastomosis / shunt vessel
  - Sites → fingertips, ear lobes
  - Role → temperature regulation
  - No capillary → no exchange
