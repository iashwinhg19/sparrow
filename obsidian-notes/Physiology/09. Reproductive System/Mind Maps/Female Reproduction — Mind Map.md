# Female Reproduction

## Oogenesis
- A → oogonia (2n)
  - B → mitosis
- C → primary oocyte (2n)
  - Meiosis I arrest till puberty
  - Diplotene stage of prophase
- D → meiosis I at puberty
- E → secondary oocyte (n)
  - Meiosis II arrest in metaphase
- F → first polar body (n)
- G → meiosis II resumes during fertilization
- H → female pronucleus (n)
- I → second polar body (n)

## Regulation
### Two cell two gonadotropin model
- Hypothalamus → GnRH → (+) anterior pituitary
- LH → theca cells → androstenedione
- FSH → granulosa cells
  - Androstenedione → aromatase → estradiol
  - Progesterone
  - Activin
  - Inhibin-B
### Feedback
- Estradiol (−) LH
- Progesterone (−) LH
- Activin (+) FSH
- Inhibin-B (−) FSH

## Menstrual cycle
- 28-day cycle in females
- Estrous cycle → mammals other than humans
### Changes in ovary — follicular phase
- FSH → follicular growth
  - 10–15 follicles grow
  - 1 follicle dominant → Graafian follicle
- Graafian follicle
  - Maximum FSH receptors
  - Maximum aromatase activity
  - Maximum estrogen production
- Estrogen → + feedback → ↑↑ LH (LH surge)
- LH surge actions
  - Acts on 1° oocyte → meiosis I completed → ovulation
  - Progesterone synthesis
  - Synthesis of prostaglandins
- LH timing
  - LH surge → 24–36 hrs before ovulation
  - LH peak → 8–10 hrs before ovulation
- FSH surge → lysis of follicular wall on day 14 → ovulation
### Changes in ovary — luteal phase
- Bleeding inside Graafian follicle → clots
- LH → luteal cells (yellow-coloured)
- Corpus luteum → progesterone
- No fertilization → degenerates on day 24 → menstrual blood loss
- Constant duration → 14 days
### Changes in endometrium
- Menstrual phase → 5 d
  - Influence of PGF2α
  - Constriction of spiral arteries → ischemia
  - Degeneration → shedding of superficial 2/3
- Proliferative phase → 6–14 d
  - Influence of estrogen
  - ↑ Endometrial thickness
  - ↑ Size of glands without secretion
- Ovulation → day 14
- Secretory phase → 15–28 d
  - Influence of progesterone
  - Max endometrial thickness
  - Uterine glands secrete clear fluid
  - Spiralling of blood vessels
### Changes in cervix
- Estrogen
  - Thin, watery → easy sperm movement
  - Spinnbarkeit → stretchable (elasticity)
  - ↑ NaCl, ↓ proteins → fern pattern
- Progesterone
  - Thick, less elastic
### Changes in vagina
- Parabasal cells → lack of hormonal activity
- Superficial cells → estrogen
- Intermediate cells → progesterone
### Basal body temperature
- ↑ 0.5–1 °C during ovulation
- Due to progesterone → thermogenic action

## Female sex hormones
### Steroid hormones
- Estrogen
- Progesterone
### Protein hormones
- Inhibin
- Activin
- Relaxin
### Estrogen
- C-18 steroid
- Sources → granulosa cells, placenta, corpus luteum
- Forms
  - Estradiol → reproductive age group
  - Estriol → pregnancy
  - Estrone → menopause
- In circulation
  - 2 % unbound → produces effects
  - 60 % bound by albumin
  - 38 % GBG / SHBG
- Actions
  - Uterus → ↑ blood flow, ↑ contractility, ↑ excitability
  - Secondary sexual → female contour, fat in breast & buttocks
  - Breast → growth, ductal proliferation
  - Bone → osteoprotegerin ⊣ RANKL → ↓ osteoclasts → ↓ resorption
  - Liver → ↓ plasma LDL
  - CVS → ⊣ platelet activation, vasodilation by ↑ NO
  - CNS → neuroprotective
  - Kidneys → salt & water retention
### Progesterone
- Uterus → anti-estrogenic (less active, excitable, contractile)
- Breast → lobulo-alveolar growth
- CNS → thermogenic, BBT ↑ 0.5–1 °C
- Respiration → stimulates → CO2 washout → ↓ alveolar PCO2
- Kidneys → natriuresis
  - Only steroid hormone causing natriuresis
### Inhibin, activin, follistatin
- Inhibin A → corpus luteum
- Inhibin B → granulosa cells
- Inhibin → negative feedback on FSH
- Activin → stimulates FSH
- Follistatin → inhibits FSH
### Relaxin
- Sources → uterus, placenta
- Relaxes pubic symphysis
- Dilates cervix
- → Aids in labour
### hCG
- Glycoprotein hormone
- Source → syncytiotrophoblast
- α-subunit → similar to FSH, LH, TSH
- β-subunit → similar to LH
- T½ → hCG 37 h vs LH 30 min
- Potency → hCG 80× LH
- Functions
  - Maintains corpus luteum up to 6 weeks
  - Leydig cells → testosterone
  - Immunosuppressive → no fetal rejection
  - Corpus luteum → relaxin

## Lactation
### Mammogenesis
- Growth of breast
- Estrogen → ductal growth
- Progesterone → lobulo-alveolar growth
### Lactogenesis
- Production of milk
- Prolactin, insulin, growth hormone
### Galactokinesis
- Release of milk
- Milk ejection / let-down reflex
  - Suckling → hypothalamus
  - → Oxytocin from posterior pituitary
  - Neuroendocrine reflex
  - Inhibited by anxiety, pain, depression
### Galactopoiesis
- Maintenance of lactation
- Prolactin
