# Gastrointestinal Motility

## Interstitial cells of Cajal
- Pacemaker of GIT
- Restless membrane potential → BER
### Location
- Stomach → mid body along greater curvature
- Among single-unit smooth muscle (gap junctions)
- Applied → GIST arises from ICC

### Basal electrical rhythm
- BER
  - Oscillates −60 to −40 mV
  - No role in muscle contraction
- Action potential / spikes
  - BER crosses threshold (−40)
  - Depolarisation → Ca²⁺ influx
  - Repolarisation → K⁺ efflux
  - Cause smooth-muscle contraction

### Effect of ANS
- PNS (acetylcholine)
  - ↑ spikes
  - ↑ force of contraction, ↑ tension
- SNS (norepinephrine)
  - ↓ spikes
  - ↓ force of contraction, ↓ tension
- BER frequency (per min)
  - Duodenum 12
  - Ileum 8
  - Stomach 4
  - Caecum 2

## Motility in GIT during fed state
### Food in mouth — deglutition
- Process of swallowing
- Oral stage → voluntary
- Pharyngeal stage → involuntary
  - Soft palate, larynx closes, epiglottis
- Oesophageal stage → involuntary
- Deglutition reflex
  - Food in mouth → stimulus
  - CN V, IX, X → afferent
  - NTS in medulla → centre
  - CN V, VII, XII → efferent
  - Pharyngeal muscle & tongue → effector
  - Swallowing → response

### Food in oesophagus — peristalsis
- Stretch → serotonin → myenteric plexus → peristalsis
- Physiologic → law of peristalsis (oral → anal)
  - Contraction behind → ACh & substance P
  - Relaxation ahead → NO & VIP
- Pathologic → reverse peristalsis (anal → oral) in vomiting
- Types
  - Primary → progressive → physiologic
  - Secondary → progressive → oesophageal irritation
  - Tertiary → non-progressive → oesophageal spasm
- Achalasia cardia
  - Absence of NO → ACh overactivity
  - → sustained LES contraction
  - Rx → botulinum toxin (inhibits ACh release)
  - Normal LES → [ACh] = [NO]

### Food in stomach — gastric emptying
- Receptive relaxation → temporary storage
- Retropulsion → mixing & grinding
- Gastric emptying → pyloric sphincter opens momentarily
- GE ↑
  - ↑ gastric volume
  - Gastrin, motilin
  - Acetylcholine, substance P, serotonin
- GE ↓
  - Duodenal carbohydrate, fat, protein, acid, hyperosmolar content
  - → enterogastric reflex (local enteric reflex)
  - NO, VIP, CCK, dopamine, enkephalin
- Gastrocolic reflex
  - Defecation in newborn after a meal
  - Stomach distension → ↑ colonic motility → defecation
  - Amplified by gastrin

### Food in intestine — small intestine
- Segmentation
  - Both ends contract → slow movement
  - → adequate time for digestion & absorption
- Motility ↑ → PNS, motilin, gastrin, CCK, serotonin, gastroileal reflex
- Motility ↓ → SNS, secretin

### Food in intestine — large intestine
- Haustrations
  - Combined circular & longitudinal contraction
  - Absorb water & electrolytes
- Mass movement
  - Moves along segment → rectal distension → ↑ motility → defecation
  - Proximal colon (1) · distal colon (2, 3, 4)

### Rectal motility — defecation reflex
- Internal anal sphincter
  - Smooth muscle, involuntary
  - PNS relaxes → facilitates defecation
  - SNS contracts → inhibits defecation
- External anal sphincter
  - Skeletal muscle, voluntary
  - Pudendal nerve
- Mechanism
  - Rectal distension → stretch afferents → spinal cord
  - PNS output → relaxes IAS
  - If favourable → abdominal, rectal, colonic contraction
  - If favourable → EAS relaxes
- Rectal pressure
  - 18 mmHg → first urge
  - 55 mmHg → voluntary control lost
  - Sudden release of IAS & EAS → soiling

### Transit time
- Oesophagus 2–3 s
- Stomach 2–5 h
- Small intestine 3–6 h
- Caecum 4 h
- Proximal 1/3 colon 6 h
- Distal 2/3 colon 9 h
- Sigmoid colon 12 h

### Dietary fibre (roughage)
- Lignin, pectin, cellulose, hemicellulose
- Sources → cereals, fruits, vegetables
- Adds bulk to diet → ↓ food intake
- Slows glucose absorption
- ↓ total & LDL cholesterol
- Adds bulk to stool → retains water
- Microflora → short-chain fatty acids
- Prevents colonic diverticula

## Motility in GIT during fasting state
### Migrating motor complex
- Fasting & interdigestive period
- Stomach → colon
- Once every 90 min, 5 cm/min
- Regulated by motilin (Mo cells) → time keeper
  - ↑ GI motility
  - Erythromycin → motilin receptor → diabetic gastroparesis
- Clears foreign bodies, bacteria, undigested food
- Phases
  - I → no activity (quiescent)
  - II → irregular activity
  - III → regular activity, maximum amplitude
