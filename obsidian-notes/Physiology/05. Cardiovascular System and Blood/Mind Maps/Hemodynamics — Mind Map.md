# Hemodynamics

## Poiseuille-Hagen law
- Blood flow ∝ (PA − PB) × r⁴ / (length × viscosity)
  - PA − PB → pressure difference
  - Number of RBC → determines viscosity
### Consequences
- 1 → blood flow ∝ r⁴
  - Renal artery stenosis → ½ r → flow reduced 16 times
- 2 → blood flow ∝ 1/L
  - 2L → flow reduced to ½
- 3 → blood flow ∝ 1/viscosity
  - ↑ RBC number → ↑ viscosity → ↓ flow (hyperviscosity)
- 4 → fourth power law: resistance ∝ 1/r⁴
  - 2r → ↓ resistance
  - ½ r → ↑ resistance
- 5 → blood flow ∝ 1/area

## Blood pressure
- Lateral pressure exerted by blood on a vessel wall
### Principle
- Bernoulli → potential energy + kinetic energy = constant
  - Potential energy = blood pressure
  - Kinetic energy = blood flow
  - → BP + blood flow = constant
### Measurement
- SI unit → pascal
- Preferred unit → mm Hg
  - SBP = 120 mm Hg
  - DBP = 80 mm Hg
- Pulse pressure = SBP − DBP
- MAP = DBP + ⅓ pulse pressure = 93–100 mm Hg
### Modes of measurement
- Direct
  - Invasive
  - Measures intra-arterial BP
  - Used in animals (research)
- Indirect
  - Non-invasive
  - Underestimates SBP by 10 mm Hg
  - Used in clinical practice → sphygmomanometer
### Korotkoff's sounds
- Phase 1 → faint tapping → SBP
- Phase 2 → murmur-like sound
- Phase 3 → crisp & clear sound
- Phase 4 → muffled sound
- Phase 5 → sound disappears → DBP
- Complete obstruction → no flow, no sound
- Little deflation → Korotkoff sound
- Complete deflation → non-constricted flow, no sound
### False BP recordings
- Small size cuff
- Obesity
- Thick & calcified vessel
- Riva-Rocci cuff
  - Length → covers 80 % of arm circumference
  - Width → covers 40 % of arm circumference
### Auscultatory gap
- Silent gap between first sounds and their return
- Significance → DBP overestimated
### Direct method — heart chambers
- Right atrium → 4 / 0 mm Hg
- Right ventricle → 25 / 5 mm Hg
- Left atrium → 12 / 4 mm Hg
- Left ventricle → 140 / 4 mm Hg
- PCWP → left atrial pressure
  - 4–12 mm Hg (average 10)

## Other parameters determining blood flow
### Reynold's number
- Laminar flow → silent
- Turbulent flow → noise present
  - Seen in aorta & pulmonary artery
  - Basis of murmurs
- ∝ density × diameter × velocity / viscosity
- Use → predicts type of blood flow
- Interpretation
  - < 2000 → laminar
  - 2000–3000 → transitional
  - > 3000 → turbulent
- Pregnancy → flow murmurs
  - Anaemia → ↓ RBC → ↓ viscosity → ↑ Reynold's number
### Laplace's law
- Pressure = 2 × tension / radius
- Tension ∝ radius
- Capillary → small radius, thin wall → doesn't rupture
- Dilated heart → ↑ radius → ↑ tension
  - Dilated cardiomyopathy → heart failure
- Aortic aneurysm → ↑ radius → ↑ tension
  - → rupture → death
