# Embryology

## Mesoderm
- Paraxial
  - Somites
  - Dermatome → dermis of back
  - Myotome → skeletal muscle (except iris and pharyngeal arch muscle origins)
  - Sclerotome → vertebrae and ribs
- Intermediate
  - Urogenital system
  - Kidneys, gonads, genital ducts
  - Adrenal cortex
- Lateral plate
  - Somatopleuric → body wall, limbs, appendicular skeleton
  - Splanchnopleuric → cardiac muscle, smooth muscle of gut, blood vessels
- Smooth muscle not from mesoderm
  - Iris sphincter and dilator → neural crest
  - Ciliary muscle → neural crest
  - Sweat and mammary gland myoepithelium → ectoderm

## Notochord and neural tube
- Notochord
  - Induces the overlying ectoderm to form the neural plate
  - Remnants → nucleus pulposus, apical ligament of dens, tectorial membrane
- Neural tube
  - Brain and spinal cord
  - Oligodendrocytes
  - Astrocytes
  - Ependymal cells
  - Retina and retinal pigment epithelium
  - Posterior pituitary
- Intervertebral disc
  - Annulus fibrosus → sclerotome
  - Nucleus pulposus → notochord

## Neural crest
- Site → junction of neural tube and surface ectoderm
- Peripheral nervous system
  - Dorsal root and autonomic ganglia
  - Enteric plexus
  - Schwann cells
  - Pia and arachnoid
  - Adrenal medulla
  - Parafollicular C cells
  - Melanocytes
- Head and neck
  - Facial bones
  - Odontoblasts → dentine
  - Pharyngeal arch cartilages
  - Dermis of face and neck
  - Ciliary and iris muscles
- Not neural crest
  - Adrenal cortex → intermediate mesoderm
  - Dura mater → mesoderm

## Diaphragm
- Septum transversum → central tendon
- Pleuroperitoneal membranes → posterolateral parts
- Dorsal mesentery of oesophagus → crura
- Body wall mesoderm → peripheral muscle rim
- Phrenic nerve C3 C4 C5 → because it starts in the neck
- Congenital diaphragmatic hernia
  - Pleuroperitoneal membrane fails to close → Bochdalek foramen
  - Left ≫ right
  - Bowel in the chest → lung hypoplasia
  - Scaphoid abdomen, bowel sounds in chest

## Pharyngeal apparatus
- Arch
  - Mesoderm core with neural crest
  - Ectoderm outside, endoderm inside
- Clefts (ectoderm)
  - 1st → external auditory meatus, outer layer of tympanic membrane
  - 2nd–4th → buried in the cervical sinus by 2nd arch overgrowth
  - Persistent cervical sinus → branchial cyst or fistula along anterior border of sternocleidomastoid
- Pouches (endoderm)
  - 1st → tympanic cavity, auditory tube, mastoid antrum, inner layer of tympanic membrane
  - 2nd → palatine tonsil
  - 3rd → thymus, inferior parathyroid
  - 4th → superior parathyroid, ultimobranchial body
  - C cells → neural crest cells that migrate into the ultimobranchial body
  - DiGeorge → 3rd and 4th pouch defect, 22q11 deletion, no thymus, hypocalcaemia
- Arch cartilages
  - 1st Meckel → malleus, incus, mandible template
  - 2nd Reichert → stapes, styloid, stylohyoid ligament, lesser cornu and upper body of hyoid
  - 3rd → greater cornu and lower body of hyoid
  - 4th and 6th → laryngeal cartilages
- Arch nerves
  - 1st → V3
  - 2nd → VII
  - 3rd → IX
  - 4th → superior laryngeal of X
  - 6th → recurrent laryngeal of X

## Arch arteries
- 1st → maxillary artery
- 2nd → stapedial and hyoid arteries
- 3rd → common carotid, proximal internal carotid
- 4th left → arch of aorta
- 4th right → proximal right subclavian
- 5th → degenerates
- 6th left → left pulmonary artery, ductus arteriosus
- 6th right → right pulmonary artery
- Other sources
  - Aortic sac → brachiocephalic trunk, part of arch
  - 7th cervical intersegmental → distal subclavian both sides
- Recurrent laryngeal nerves
  - Left hooks the ductus / ligamentum arteriosum → long course
  - Right hooks the subclavian because right 6th and distal right 4th arch vanish → short course
- Anomalies
  - Aberrant right subclavian → right 4th arch lost, right dorsal aorta persists, dysphagia lusoria
  - Double aortic arch → both 4th arches and both dorsal aortae persist, vascular ring

## Veins
- Vitelline → hepatic sinusoids, portal vein, hepatic part of IVC
- Umbilical
  - Right disappears
  - Left → ductus venosus → ligamentum venosum, ligamentum teres
- Anterior cardinal → internal jugular, left brachiocephalic
- Common cardinal right → SVC
- Posterior cardinal → common iliac veins, azygos root
- Subcardinal → renal, gonadal, renal segment of IVC
- Supracardinal → azygos, hemiazygos, infrarenal IVC
- Sinus venosus right horn → smooth part of right atrium
- Sinus venosus left horn → coronary sinus

## Mesogastrium
- Ventral
  - Falciform ligament
  - Lesser omentum
  - Coronary and triangular ligaments of liver
- Dorsal
  - Greater omentum
  - Gastrosplenic ligament
  - Lienorenal ligament
  - Gastrophrenic ligament

## Midgut
- Normal → 270° counter-clockwise around the SMA
- Non-rotation → small bowel right, colon left
- Reversed rotation → clockwise, transverse colon behind the SMA
- Mixed rotation → caecum below the liver, Ladd's bands over duodenum
- Incomplete rotation → subhepatic caecum
- Volvulus risk → narrow mesenteric base

## Abdominal wall defects
- Omphalocele
  - Midgut fails to return at week 10
  - Covered by amnion and peritoneum
  - Umbilical cord at the apex
  - Many associated anomalies, higher mortality
- Gastroschisis
  - Defect to the right of the umbilicus
  - No covering
  - Bowel thickened, matted
  - Few associated anomalies, intestinal obstruction

## Genital ducts
- Mesonephric (Wolffian)
  - Male → epididymis, vas deferens, seminal vesicle, ejaculatory duct, trigone, ureteric bud
  - Male remnant → appendix of epididymis
  - Female remnant → Gartner's duct, epoophoron, paroophoron
- Paramesonephric (Müllerian)
  - Female → fallopian tubes, uterus, cervix, upper vagina
  - Male remnant → appendix of testis, prostatic utricle
- Genital ridge
  - Testis
  - Ovary
