# Thyroid Gland

## Thyroid Gland — Overview
### Location
- Front of neck, on the trachea
- Parathyroid glands on posterior surface

### Histology
- Colloid inside follicles
- Follicular cells → T3, T4
- Parafollicular C cells → calcitonin

## Thyroid Hormone
### Synthesis — raw materials
- Tyrosine → from thyroglobulin (Tg)
- Iodine → present as iodide in diet

### Synthesis — follicular cell
- Basolateral end
  - Na⁺–I⁻ symporter → I⁻ in
  - Na⁺-K⁺ ATPase (3Na⁺ out, 2K⁺ in) → low Na⁺ inside
- Apical end
  - Pendrin → I⁻ out to lumen, Cl⁻ in
  - TPO → I⁻ (reduced) → I₂ (oxidized)
  - Organification onto thyroglobulin
- Coupling reactions
  - MIT + DIT = T3
  - DIT + DIT = T4
  - DIT + MIT = rT3 (inactive)
- Storage in colloid → reserve 2–3 months

### Mechanism — NIS
- Location → basolateral end of follicular cells
- Function → iodide trapping
- Type → 2° active transport
- Other locations → salivary, mammary gland, placenta
- Wolff–Chaikoff effect
  - Excess iodide → inhibits organification → ↓ T3, T4
  - Use → Lugol's iodine before thyroid surgery

### Mechanism — pendrin
- Iodide–chloride antiporter
- Locations → thyroid gland, inner ear
- Mutation → Pendred syndrome
  - Goiter
  - Sensorineural hearing loss

### Mechanism — TPO
- Reduced iodide → oxidised I₂ → T3, T4
- Blocked by anti-thyroid drugs
  - Propylthiouracil
  - Carbimazole
  - Methimazole

### Regulation
- Negative feedback (TRH → TSH → T3, T4)
- TSH actions
  - ↑ vascularity of gland
  - ↑ iodide trapping
  - → ↑ T3, T4 synthesis

### Binding proteins
- Colloid breakdown → T3, T4 → blood
- 99 % bound
  - Albumin
  - TBG → highest affinity
  - Pre-albumin / transthyretin → binds T3, T4
- 1 % free → responsible for actions

### Receptor
- T3 → MCT (monocarboxylate transporter)
- T4 → OATP (organic anion transporting polypeptide)
- → bind nuclear receptor in target cell

### T3 vs T4
- Potency → T3 most, T4 least
- Half-life → T3 1 day, T4 7 days
- Protein binding → T3 less, T4 tight
- Action → T3 rapid, T4 long acting
- Colloid quantity → T3 less, T4 more
- Form → T3 physiological, T4 pharmacological (drug)

### Peripheral conversion
- T4 → deiodinase → T3
- Deiodinase contains selenocysteine (21st amino acid)

### Actions 1–3 (mediated by T3)
- (1) ↑ BMR
  - ↑ O₂ consumption everywhere except
    - Adult brain, testes, uterus, spleen
  - ↑ end products → vasodilation → ↓ PVR
- (2) Physiologic uncoupler
  - T3 → UCP1 → heat dissipation → thermogenic
  - Hyperthyroid → ↑↑ heat → heat intolerance
  - Hypothyroid → no heat → cold intolerance
- (3) Catabolism
  - Glycogenolysis → ↑ blood glucose
  - Lipolysis → ↑ free fatty acids
  - Proteolysis
  - ↓ blood cholesterol
  - Hypothyroid → ↑ cholesterol → atherosclerosis

### Actions 4–6
- (4) GAGs
  - T3 prevents GAG accumulation (hyaluronic acid, chondroitin)
  - Hypothyroid → GAGs bind water → myxedema (puffy face)
- (5) Development
  - Brain growth → ↑ myelination in CNS
  - Bone growth
  - Congenital hypothyroidism
    - Mental retardation (m/c reversible cause) → low IQ
    - Stunted growth
- (6) Heart
  - ↑ responsiveness to catecholamines (NE)
  - → ↑ HR, ↑ SV, ↑ contractility (β-receptor)
  - Thyroid storm → ↑ NE action → Rx propranolol

### Actions 7–9
- (7) Lungs
  - ↑ metabolism → ↑ O₂ use → hypoxia
  - → ODC shift to right
  - → stimulates respiration → ↑ respiratory rate
- (8) GIT
  - ↑ motility & ↑ secretions
  - Hypothyroid → ↓ motility → constipation
- (9) Reproductive system
  - Permissive role in reproduction
  - Liver → ↑ sex steroid binding globulin
