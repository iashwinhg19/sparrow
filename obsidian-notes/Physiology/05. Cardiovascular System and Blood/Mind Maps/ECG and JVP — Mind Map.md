# ECG and JVP

## Electrocardiography (ECG)
### ECG leads
- Measures potential difference b/w (+) & (−) electrodes
  - → deflections → represented as waves
- 12 leads
  - 6 limb leads
    - Bipolar → lead I, II, III
    - Unipolar (augmented) → aVR, aVL, aVF
  - 6 chest leads → V1–V6

### Limb leads
- Bipolar leads
  - Einthoven's triangle
  - Lead I → R arm (−) to L arm (+)
  - Lead II → R arm (−) to L foot (+)
  - Lead III → L arm (−) to L foot (+)
  - Einthoven's law → lead II = lead I + lead III
- Augmented unipolar leads
  - AKA Goldberger's leads
  - One limb (+), remaining 2 limbs (−)
  - Potentials augmented 1.5 times
  - aVR (+ R arm) · aVL (+ L arm) · aVF (+ L foot)

### Clinical significance
- Leads represent the cardiac activity of the wall they face
- V3, V4 → anterior wall → LAD
- V1, V2 → septal wall → LAD
- aVL, I, V5, V6 → lateral wall → LCA circumflex branch
- II, III, aVF → inferior wall → RCA
- LAD → widow maker's artery → m/c artery causing MI

### Normal ECG
- ECG paper
  - Small square → 0.04 s, 0.1 mV
  - Large square = 25 small squares → 0.20 s, 0.5 mV
  - x-axis time, y-axis amplitude
- Waveform
  - P → atrial depolarisation
  - PR segment → A-V nodal delay
  - PR interval → 0.12–0.22 s (figure)
  - QRS → ventricular depolarisation, < 0.12 s
  - J point → junction of QRS and ST
  - T → ventricular repolarisation
  - U → late repolarisation of Purkinje fibres, papillary muscles
  - QT → total ventricular activity = depol + repol, 0.40 s
  - RR → distance between R waves
- Colour key
  - Waves → yellow
  - Segments → blue → isoelectric lines, no current flow
  - Intervals → green
- Applied aspect
  - ST elevation / depression → ischaemic / infarcted tissue
  - PR interval > 0.20 s → heart block
  - QT changes with HR → Bazett: QTc = QT / √RR
  - RR → HR = 1500 / small squares b/w 2 R waves

### Cardiac axis
- Direction of current flow
  - Whole heart → septum → endocardium → epicardium
    - Depolarisation and repolarisation in opposite directions
  - Individual myocardial cell → both in the same direction
- Direction of overall electrical activity
  - Leads towards mean QRS axis → (+) deflection
  - Leads away from mean QRS axis (aVR) → (−) deflection
  - Mean QRS axis → towards left ventricle (↑ muscle mass), 59°

### His bundle ECG
- Recorded along with routine ECG
- Significance → conduction blocks → intervals prolonged
- A → AV nodal activity
- H → His bundle activity
- V → ventricular depolarisation
- Intervals → PA, AH, HV

## Jugular venous pressure (JVP)
- Recorded in internal jugular vein
### Waves
- a → right atrial contraction
- c → right ventricular contraction + tricuspid bulging into right atrium
- v → right atrial filling
### Descents
- x → right atrial relaxation
- y → right atrial emptying
