# Exercise Physiology

## Significance and type of exercise
### Significance
- Release of endorphins → mood enhancers
- CPET → diagnose heart & lung diseases
- Lifestyle modification → treatment for diabetes mellitus
### Isotonic
- Muscle tension → constant
- Muscle length → changes
- Example → running on a treadmill
### Isometric
- Muscle tension → changes
- Muscle length → constant
- Example → pushing a wall

## Sources of energy
### Phosphocreatine / phosphagen system
- ATP from stored creatine phosphate
- Duration → 8–10 sec
- 100 m sprints
### Glycogen lactic acid system
- Anaerobic ATP production
  - → ↑ lactic acid → fatigue
- Duration → 1.3–1.6 min
### Aerobic system
- Unlimited energy (ATP) for unlimited time
### Endurance
- Carbohydrate-rich diet → ↑ muscle glycogen
  - → energy for up to 4 hrs
  - → improves endurance

## Changes during exercise
### Skeletal muscle changes
- Contraction generates tension
- Maximum contractile force → 3–4 kg/cm²
- Blood flow 3 → 80 ml/100 g/min (↑ 20×)
### Organ blood flow
- ↓ Renal & splanchnic (GIT)
- ↑ Heart & skeletal muscles
  - → redistribution
- Cerebral → always constant (autoregulation)
- Skin → initial ↓ → later ↑ (heat dissipation)
### Exercise hyperemia
- ↑ Metabolism → local H⁺, K⁺, lactate
  - → vasodilation → ↑ blood flow → hyperemia
### Cardiac output
- ↑ 7 fold in athletes
- ↑ 4 fold in non-athletes
- CO = SV × HR
  - HR → constantly increases
  - SV → reaches plateau
  - ↑ CO due to ↑ HR
### Anticipatory tachycardia
- ↑ HR before starting exercise
- Joint proprioceptors → impulse → brain
### Blood pressure
- Isotonic
  - SBP ↑, DBP ↓
  - Metabolic end products → vasodilation → ↓ TPR
- Isometric
  - SBP ↑, DBP ↑
  - Sustained contraction → ↑ tension
  - → compresses arterioles → ↑ TPR
### Preload
- SNS stimulation → norepinephrine
  - → venoconstriction
  - → ↑ mean systemic filling pressure
  - → ↑ preload
### Respiratory changes
- ↑ RR & pulmonary ventilation
  - Muscles release K⁺ → chemoreceptors
  - ↑ Body temperature
  - Blood gases (pO2, pCO2, pH) remain normal
- Anticipatory tachypnea
  - ↑ RR before exercise
  - Proprioceptors in joints
- ↑ Oxygen consumption
  - Rest → 250 ml/min = 1 MET
  - Exercise → ↑ MET
  - Peak VO2 → O2 consumed at end of exercise
  - VO2 max → maximum possible (theoretical)
- O2 debt
  - Onset of exercise
  - Borrowing from Hb & myoglobin stores
- EPOC
  - Excess post-exercise O2 consumption
  - ↑ RR & O2 use after exercise
  - → replenish Hb & myoglobin stores
- Diffusion of gases
  - Release of O2 to tissues
  - ODC shifts to right
- A–V O2 difference ↑
  - Tissues extract more O2

## Effects of smoking
- ↓ Exercise capacity
- ↓ Work capacity
- Toxic chemicals in smoke → constriction of airways
  - ↑ Airflow resistance
  - Ciliary paralysis
  - Swelling of epithelial cells
  - ↑ Fluid secretion into bronchial tree
