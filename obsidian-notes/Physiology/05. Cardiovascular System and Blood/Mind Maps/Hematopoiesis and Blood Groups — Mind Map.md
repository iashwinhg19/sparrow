# Hematopoiesis and Blood Groups

## Hematopoiesis
### Timelines
- Intrauterine life
  - 3 weeks – 3 months → yolk sac
  - 3 – 5 months (hepatic stage) → liver / spleen (physiological)
  - 5 – 9 months → red bone marrow, long & flat bones
- Birth – 20 years → red bone marrow, long & flat bones
- > 20 years → red bone marrow, flat midline bones (iliac crest)

### Erythropoiesis — stages
- PHSC
  - GATA + Friend of GATA (FOG) → transcription factors
- BFU-E → earliest committed progenitor
- CFU-E → highest erythropoietin receptors
- Proerythroblast → Hb synthesis begins
- Basophilic erythroblast
- Polychromatophilic erythroblast → Hb appears
- Orthochromatic erythroblast → Hb synthesis complete
- Reticulocyte → immature RBC
- Erythrocyte → mature RBC, ~7.2 µm

### Regulation of erythropoiesis
- Hypoxia → HIF acts as a transcription factor
  - Liver
  - Kidney (major source) → interstitial cells
  - Brain
  - → ↑ erythropoietin (major regulator)
  - → RBC progenitors via JAK2–STAT receptor → erythropoiesis
- Normoxia + VHL → HIF–VHL complex → degradation
- HIF = hypoxia inducible factor
- VHL = von Hippel–Lindau
- Promoting factors
  - EPO
  - Growth hormone
  - Cortisol
  - Thyroid hormone (T3)
  - Testosterone
- Inhibiting factor
  - Oestrogen → females ↓ RBC count

### Clinical significance of EPO
- Polycythaemia vera
  - JAK2 gain-of-function mutation → ↑↑ RBCs
  - Rx → ruxolitinib (JAK2 inhibitor)
- Drug forms → epoetin alfa, darbepoetin alfa
  - Anaemia of chronic disease (CKD)
  - Dialysis
- Blood doping → athletes ↑ RBC count

### Leucopoiesis
- Granulocytes → neutrophil, eosinophil, basophil
- Agranulocytes → monocyte, lymphocyte
- PHSC → lymphoid stem cell → lymphoblast → lymphocyte
- PHSC → CFU-GM → myeloblast → promyelocyte
  - → myelocyte → metamyelocyte → neutrophil / eosinophil / basophil
- CFU-GM → monoblast → promonocyte → monocyte
- Factors stimulating
  - CSF → filgrastim → Rx neutropenia
  - GM-CSF → sargramostim → Rx neutropenia
  - G-CSF
  - M-CSF
  - IL-5 → eosinophils
  - IL-3, 4 → basophils
  - TNF

### Thrombopoiesis
- PHSC → GM-CSF thrombocyte → megakaryocyte → platelets
- Thrombopoietin → eltrombopag, romiplostim
- IL-11 → oprelvekin

## Blood groups
### ABO system
- Based on antigens & oligosaccharides on RBC
- Antigens → agglutinogens
- Antibodies → agglutinins
- Antigens → H, A, B
- Sugars
  - N-acetyl galactosamine (NAG) → A
  - Galactose (GAL) → B
- Types
  - A → NAG on H → anti-B
  - B → GAL on H → anti-A
  - AB → co-dominance → universal recipient → no antibody
  - O → H only → universal donor → anti-A, anti-B
  - Bombay → no H antigen
- Landsteiner's law
  - Antigen on RBC → its antibody absent from serum
- Antigen sites
  - RBC, saliva, amniotic fluid
  - Semen, intestinal bacteria, food

### Rh system
- Antigens → C, D, E
- Rh+ → D antigen present
- Rh− → D antigen absent
- Clinical significance
  - Rh− mother + Rh+ baby
  - Fetal blood enters maternal circulation
  - Maternal antibodies against fetal RBC
  - Haemolysis in subsequent pregnancy
  - Erythroblastosis fetalis / Rh incompatibility
  - ↑ unconjugated bilirubin → crosses BBB
  - Deposited in basal ganglia → kernicterus
- Rx → anti-Rh antibodies

### Other blood group systems
- Kell absent → McLeod phenotype
  - Acanthocytes (spikes)
  - Cardiac defects
- P system
  - Autoantibodies against P → paroxysmal cold haemoglobinuria
  - Receptor for parvovirus B19, E. coli
- Duffy system
  - Receptor for Plasmodium vivax → malaria
