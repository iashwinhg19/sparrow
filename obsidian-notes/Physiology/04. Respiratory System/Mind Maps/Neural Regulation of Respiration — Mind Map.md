# Neural Regulation of Respiration

## Regulation of Respiration
- Neural → respiratory centres + pulmonary reflexes
- Chemical → change in [O2], [CO2], [H+]

## Neural Regulation
### Respiratory centre
- Pneumotaxic centre (upper pons)
  - Inhibits inspiration
  - Regulates respiratory rate (alternates inspiration & expiration)
  - ⊖ apneustic centre
- Apneustic centre (lower pons)
  - Prolongs inspiration
  - ⊕ DRG
- Pre-Bötzinger complex (medulla)
  - Pacemaker → respiratory pattern generation
  - Initiates respiration
  - Maintains spontaneous breathing during sleep
- DRG (medulla)
  - Ramp signal → lung
- VRG (medulla)
  - Overdrive phenomenon

### Applied aspect
- Overdrive phenomenon
  - Exercise → extra drive (VRG ⊕) → forceful expiration
- Ondine curse
  - No Pre-Bötzinger complex → no spontaneous breathing in sleep
  - Rx → mechanical ventilation during sleep
- Injury between medulla & spinal cord
  - No spontaneous breathing (disrupted impulse)
  - Hanging, direct cervical injury

### Respiratory pathway
- Initiation
  - Pre-Bötzinger → phrenic nerve (C3, C4, C5)
  - → contracts diaphragm → initiates inspiration
- Maintenance
  - Apneustic ⊕ DRG → ramp signals
  - → smooth sequential ↑ tidal volume up to 500 ml → lung stretch
- Termination
  - Lung stretch → stretch reflex → vagal afferent ⊖ apneustic
  - Pneumotaxic ⊖ apneustic

### Waveform in lesions
- A → complete transection above pons
  - All respiratory areas intact → normal
- B → mid-pontine section + vagus cut
  - Apneustic overactivity (apneusis) → prolonged inspiration
- C → midway between pons and medulla
  - Initiation present, regularisation absent → irregular
- D → complete transection below medulla
  - Death inevitable → flat line

## Pulmonary Reflexes
### Hering–Breuer inflation reflex
- Prevents lung injury from overstretching
- Prolonged inspiration → inspiration stops → expiration begins
- Myelinated vagal fibres (slow adapting)

### Hering–Breuer deflation reflex
- Prevents lung collapse
- Prolonged expiration → expiration stops → inspiration begins
- Myelinated vagal fibres (slow adapting)

### Head's paradoxical reflex
- Replaces fluid with air in fetal (newborn) lung
- Prolonged inspiration → further ↑↑ inspiration

### J reflex
- Pulmonary oedema → J (juxtacapillary) receptors near vessels
- → apnoea, breathlessness, hypotension, bradycardia
- Unmyelinated vagal fibres → slow C fibres

## Altered Breathing Patterns
### Normopnoea
- Normal respiration

### Cheyne–Stokes respiration
- Gradual hyperpnoea → CO2 washout
- Gradual hypopnoea → CO2 build-up
- Apnoea → complete build-up of CO2
- Physiologic → sleep
- Pathologic → congestive cardiac failure, uraemia

### Biot's (ataxic) breathing
- Hyperpnoea → abrupt apnoea → hyperpnoea
- Damage to medulla
- Meningitis

### Kussmaul's (acidotic) breathing
- Hyperventilation → ↑ rate & depth
- Diabetic ketoacidosis
