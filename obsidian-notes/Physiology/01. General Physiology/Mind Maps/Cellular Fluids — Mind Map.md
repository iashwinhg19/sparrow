# Cellular Fluids

## Total Body Water
- Water = maximum contributor of body / cell
### Distribution of body water
- Total body weight
  - 50–60 % TBW
  - 18 % proteins
  - 15 % fats
  - 7 % minerals
- TBW → ICF + ECF
  - ICF → 2/3 of TBW
  - ECF → 1/3 of TBW
    - Transcellular fluid → lubrication
    - Plasma → in blood vessels
    - Interstitial fluid → between cells
- Volumes (70 kg)
  - TBW 42 L → 60 %
  - ICF 28 L → 40 %
  - ECF 14 L → 20 %
  - Plasma 3.5 L
  - IF 10.5 L
  - TF 1–2 L
- Children → 75 % water
  - Males 60 %, females 50 %
  - Diarrhoea → lose more water → dehydration

### Factors affecting TBW
- Males → ↓ adipose, ↑ lean body mass, ↑ TBW
- Females → ↑ adipose, ↓ lean body mass, ↓ TBW

### Lean body mass
- Form of fat-free mass
- LBM = body mass − (adipose + non-adipose tissue + bone)
- LBM ∝ TBW
- Water content of LBM → 70 mL / 100 g

## Measurements of Body Fluids
- Stewart Hamilton / indicator dilution method
  - Inject indicator → measure its concentration
- A = C × V → V = A / C
- Modified → (injected − excreted) / concentration
### Indicators
- TBW → deuterium oxide, tritium oxide
- ECF → inulin (m/c), sucrose, mannitol, Na thiosulphate
- Plasma volume → radiolabelled albumin, Evans blue
- Blood volume → chromium-tagged RBCs
### Indirect calculation
- Interstitial fluid → no indicator
  - ECF = PV + IF → IF = ECF − PV
- Intracellular fluid → no indicator
  - TBW = ECF + ICF → ICF = TBW − ECF
### Notes
- BV = PV / (1 − haematocrit)
- Blood volume in child → 70 mL/kg

## Fluid Compartments
- Contain water and ions
### Ions
- ECF
  - Cation → Na⁺
  - Anion → Cl⁻
  - pH 7.4
- ICF
  - Cations → K⁺ > Mg²⁺
  - Anions → phosphates > protein anions
  - pH 7
### Water loss
- Sensible → sweating 100 mL/day (m/c exercise)
- Insensible → 600–700 mL/day
  - By evaporation → skin, lung
### Plasma osmolarity
- 300 mosm/L
- Mole → gram molecular weight (NaCl = 58.5)
- Equivalent → GMW / valency (Ca²⁺ = 20)
- Na⁺ → 270 mosm/L → major contributor
- Proteins → 0.75 mosm/L → minor
  - High molecular weight
  - Low concentration
### Tonicity (RBC placed in)
- Isotonic (0.9 % NaCl) → no change in shape
- Hypotonic (300 mmol/L urea) → water enters → swells & bursts
- Hypertonic (300 mmol/L mannitol) → water out → shrinks
### Factors preventing edema
- Edema = fluid accumulation in interstitial space
- Distensibility of compartment → SF 3 mm Hg
  - Interstitial space normally less distensible
- ↑ Lymphatic flow
  - Inflammation → protein leak from vessels
  - ↑ Lymph flow → SF 7 mm Hg
  - Washout of proteins → SF 7 mm Hg
- Total SF = 3 + 7 + 7 = 17 mm Hg

## Darrow Yannet Diagram
- Altered plasma osmolarity & volume → diseases
- Y axis → osmolarity (300 mOsm/L)
- X axis → litres (ICF 2/3, ECF 1/3)
### Saline infusion
- 0.9 % NaCl, treatment of shock
- ECF volume ↑, osmolarity no change
- ICF no change
### Diarrhea
- Loss of 0.9 % NaCl
- ECF volume ↓, osmolarity no change
- ICF no change
### Hypertension
- ↑ NaCl in ECF (hypertonic) → fluid ICF → ECF
- ECF volume ↑, osmolarity ↑
- ICF volume ↓, osmolarity ↑
### Adrenal insufficiency
- Hypo-osmotic volume contraction
- Loss of aldosterone → loss of Na⁺ → ↓ ECF osmolarity
- → fluid moves ECF → ICF
- ECF volume ↓, osmolarity ↓
- ICF volume ↑, osmolarity ↓
