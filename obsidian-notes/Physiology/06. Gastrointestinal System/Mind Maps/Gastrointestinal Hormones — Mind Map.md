# Gastrointestinal Hormones

## Introduction — classification and enteroendocrine cells
- GI hormones act on the GI tract and the brain
### Three families
- Gastrin family
  - Gastrin
  - Cholecystokinin (CCK)
- Secretin family
  - Secretin
  - Glucagon
  - Vasoactive intestinal peptide (VIP)
  - Gastric inhibitory peptide (GIP)
- Others
  - Motilin
  - Guanylin
  - Peptide YY
  - Ghrelin

### Enteroendocrine cells
- G cells → gastrin
- I cells → cholecystokinin
- S cells → secretin
- K cells → GIP
- Mo cells → motilin
- M cells ≠ Mo cells
  - M = microfold cells of Peyer's patches (terminal ileum)

## Gastrin family
### Gastrin
- Secretion loop
  - Antral distention (eating) → G cells → gastrin
  - → ↑ HCl → pepsinogen → pepsin → protein digestion
  - Pepsin (+) → more gastrin
- Release ↑
  - Antral distention
  - Protein-rich diet (↑ pepsin)
  - GRP from vagal nerve endings
  - Ca²⁺
  - Epinephrine
- Release ↓
  - Excess acid
  - Somatostatin (universal inhibitor)
  - GIP
  - Secretin
- Actions
  - ↑ acid production via CCK-B receptor
  - LES contraction → prevents reflux (protective)
  - ↑ gastric motility
  - ↑ pepsin levels
  - Trophic → GI mucosal growth

### CCK-PZ
- Fat- and protein-rich food → I cells → CCK-PZ
- Specific actions — CCK (CCK-A receptor)
  - Cholagogue → gall-bladder contraction
  - Sphincter of Oddi relaxation → bile release
- Specific actions — PZ
  - Enzyme-rich pancreatic juice
- GI actions
  - Augments secretin
  - ↑ intestinal & colonic motility
  - Inhibits gastric emptying
- CNS actions
  - ↑ satiety → ↓ food intake
  - Implicated in anxiety
  - Aids analgesia

## Secretin family
### Secretin
- AKA natural antacid
- First hormone discovered (Bayliss & Starling)
- Acid-rich chyme → duodenum → S cells → secretin
- Actions
  - HCO₃⁻-rich (alkaline) pancreatic juice → acid neutralisation
  - Augments CCK
  - ↓ gastric acid secretion
  - Contracts pyloric sphincter → holds food in stomach

### GIP
- Now glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide
- Oral glucose → K cells → GIP → β cells of islets → ↑ insulin
- Incretins → GI hormones that ↑ insulin
  - GIP
  - Glucagon-like peptide (GLP)

### VIP
- Salivary glands → potentiates ACh
- Stomach → ↓ acid production
- Intestine
  - ↑ fluid & electrolyte secretion
  - Smooth-muscle relaxation
- VIPoma → neuroendocrine tumour, ↑ VIP
  - WDHA = pancreatic cholera = Verner–Morrison syndrome
  - Watery diarrhoea (↑ fluid/electrolyte secretion)
  - Hypokalaemia (↑ K⁺ secretion)
  - Achlorhydria

## Other hormones
### Motilin
- Seen in all fasting individuals
- ↑ GI motility → regulates MMC (stomach → colon)
- Erythromycin = motilin receptor agonist
  - → treats diabetic gastroparesis

### Guanylin
- ↑ Cl⁻ secretion in GI tract
- Similar to E. coli toxin → molecular mimicry
  - E. coli acts through guanylin receptor

### Peptide YY
- Stimulus → fat-rich diet
- Stomach → ↓ gastric motility, ↓ acid secretion
- Ileum → ↓ ileal motility (ileal brake)

### Ghrelin
- Hunger hormone → hunger contractions
- From oxyntic glands of stomach
- Peaks during fasting
- High in anorexia nervosa, low in obesity
- Actions
  - Orexinergic → ↑ food intake
  - ↑ gastric acid, ↑ gastric motility
  - ↑ adipogenesis
  - ↑ GH release → GH-REL-IN (releasing)
